## Written to suit each platform's ranking

These are widely observed practices, not guarantees. Nobody outside the platforms knows the ranking rules, so treat every item as a test and read the results at day 14 and day 30.

**LinkedIn**
- Line one carries the insight, because the fold cuts at roughly 200 characters.
- Carousels are uploaded as PDF documents, one idea per slide, so people swipe through and spend time on the post.
- No outbound link in the post body. If a link is needed, it goes in the first comment.
- At most three hashtags, at the end.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour. Replies are real conversation, not engagement bait.
- The founder's personal profile shares each page post with a line of its own, since a new page has little reach by itself.

**X**
- The first post of a thread must stand alone. A thread is useful only if post 1 is worth reading without it.
- Native video and images. No links in the main post.
- Hashtags: none, or one.
- Replies on other accounts' posts in the same niche (fund operators, ESOP, cap tables) bring more profile visits than extra posts do.

## Follower growth, without bait

1. Pin the strongest post on each page. Pin P1 now, then replace it with P3 after the 18th.
2. Put the page link in the founders' LinkedIn profiles and X bios.
3. Use LinkedIn's "invite to follow" on connections who engage with a post.
4. End posts with a real question or a clean statement. Never "comment YES".
5. Keep the weekly mix: five teaching posts for every product post. A feed that only sells does not get followed.

## What to log for every post

Impressions, saves or bookmarks, comments, profile visits and net new followers, plus the first-line hook used. At day 14, keep the formats that earned saves and follows and drop the ones that earned only impressions. Do not report a benchmark we have not measured.
