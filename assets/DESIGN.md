## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the calculated elegance of high-tier sovereign enterprise and high-growth venture capital in the GCC region. It balances deep institutional authority with sharp, precision-engineered fintech momentum. The visual signature merges architectural minimalism with high-end financial tactility—evoking uncompromising trust, strategic exclusivity, and clarity.

Key style characteristics:
- **Spatial Purity:** Replaces dated, muddy sand-yellow tints with crisp porcelain whites, layered slate surfaces, and intentional architectural whitespace.
- **Controlled Radiance:** Strategic deployment of champagne gold and electric amber strictly reserved for conversion triggers, premium tier indicators, and high-value yield states.
- **Data Veracity:** Strict micro-typography rules and crisp geometry delivering clarity for balance sheets, feasibility matrices, and algorithmic projections.
- **Bilingual Equilibrium:** Seamless parity between Arabic (RTL) and Latin/English (LTR) typography, prioritizing optical weight balance without compromising calligraphic integrity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate institutional tone via deep obsidian navy, accented by metallic champagne gold for high-intent conversions and emerald for financial performance.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces:** The default canvas is pure porcelain (`#FFFFFF`), with layered card foundations built on pristine slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`) and slate-100 (`#F1F5F9`). This removes all muddy parchment artifacts.
- **Brand & Structural Dark (Primary):** Obsidian Blue (`#0B132B`) grounds high-level navigation, hero sections, executive dashboards, and core transactional headers.
- **Conversion & Value Accent (Secondary):** Deep Champagne Gold (`#D97706`) paired with Electric Amber (`#F59E0B`) highlights purchase triggers, priority badges, and active state indicators.
- **Growth & Performance Indicator (Tertiary):** Sovereign Emerald (`#10B981`) signifies verified ROI, upward trends, and positive variance indicators.
- **Typography & Structural Lines:** Text rests at midnight slate (`#0F172A`) for effortless legibility, supported by polished slate (`#475569`) for secondary narrative. Structural rules utilize precise slate borders (`#E2E8F0`).

## Typography

The type scale relies on IBM Plex Sans (and its unified Arabic counterpart IBM Plex Sans Arabic) to project institutional stability with crisp mechanical clarity. It bypasses overly expressive or casual scripts in favor of engineered legibility.

### Implementation Guidelines
- **RTL Alignment:** In Arabic mode, text lines strictly left-to-right (for numeric financial strings) and right-to-left for analytical copy. Ensure tabular figures (`tnum`) are enabled across all financial tables, metrics, and currency designations.
- **Optical Balance:** Arabic letterforms naturally command slightly more vertical presence; maintain a minimum line-height ratio of 1.45x for Arabic body text to eliminate diacritic collision.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Use Western Arabic numerals (`0-9`) consistently across investment calculators, currency displays (e.g., `SAR 120,000`), and growth percentages to maintain institutional consistency.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column grid system anchored by strict margins to establish rhythm for analytical content.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grids
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column layout, max-width `1280px`, with `margin: 3rem` outer margins and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Multi-step financial wizards and feasibility outputs collapse into vertical stacked single-column units.

### Spacing Strategy
- Spacing relies on a base-8 rhythmic progression.
- Component padding strictly scales from `space-sm` (dense tables, interactive tooltips) to `space-xl` (hero sections, pricing cards, executive showcases).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach: low-contrast geometric outlines paired with deep, tinted ambient shadows. This prevents the interfaces from feeling floaty or flat, reinforcing physical structure and precision.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Surfaces rest at `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` without shadow, defined purely by continuous `1px` borders in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Elevation):** Standard cards and catalog items. A subtle perimeter border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) backed by a diffused drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active & Hover Cards):** Product showcase cards and pricing tiers during active hover: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.08)`, border changes to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Modals & Floating Tools):** AI tracking sidebars, checkout panels, and feasibility calculators: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.14)`.
- **Conversion Glow (Accent Elevation):** Primary conversion elements use a targeted amber aura: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.30)` on primary CTAs to maximize visual pull.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`, where base elements sit at `0.5rem / 8px`). This avoids juvenile bubble-like interfaces while softening the clinical edge of enterprise software.

### Shape Scales
- **Inputs, Buttons, and Chips:** Set to `0.5rem` (8px) for clean alignment with data inputs.
- **Product Cards & Panels:** Set to `1rem` (16px) for generous interior padding and spatial containment.
- **Hero Containers & Purchase Highlights:** Set to `1.5rem` (24px) for expansive, premium framing.
- **Badges and Status Tags:** Pill style (`9999px`) to visually differentiate status data from interactive containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (High-Conversion):** Filled with solid Champagne/Amber (`#D97706`), text in `#FFFFFF`, with an interactive hover transitioning to `#B45309` and an ambient gold glow (`0 8px 20px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.35)`). Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Typography: `label-md`.
- **Executive Primary (Institutional):** Obsidian Navy (`#0B132B`) fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` text. Used for analytical exports, logins, and corporate tools.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. Hover: background shifts to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Premium Tier Badges:** Pill-shaped, subtle semi-opaque gold tint (`background: rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`), text: `#B45309`, typography: `label-sm`.
- **Financial Growth Tags:** Crisp sovereign emerald background tint (`background: rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`), text: `#047857`, displaying dynamic values (e.g., `+34.8% IRR`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.5rem`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#0B132B` with an outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.08)`. Never use muddy yellow focus states.

### Luxury Product Showcase Cards
- **Architecture:** Background `#FFFFFF`, perimeter border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `1.5rem`, corner radius `1rem`. Includes a defined card-header displaying the product asset icon, an authoritative category label in `#64748B`, and a high-contrast pricing tag (`SAR` notation in `label-sm` followed by numerical value in `title-lg`).
- **Footer Section:** Border-top `1px solid #F1F5F9`, featuring ROI performance indicators on the right (in RTL) and a primary conversion trigger on the left.

### Financial Metrics & Feasibility Display Cards
- High-density information blocks using subtle slate backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`), crisp numeric metric typography (`metric-xl`), and integrated trend arrows colored with Growth Emerald (`#10B981`).
