# Angelsta financial model, 2027 to 2031 (v3.0)

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `Angelsta_Financial_Model_2027-2031_v3.0.xlsx` | 28-sheet operational model in the Btakka layout: monthly for 60 months, three statements, funding, valuation, stress tests, revenue by market |
| `Angelsta_Pricing_Strategy.docx` | Freemium pricing strategy for founders and investors, monthly and annual, with local-currency price lists, 13 charts and infographics, and references |
| `Angelsta_Competitor_and_Comparables_Analysis.docx` | 80 competitors and alternatives across Egypt, Qatar, GCC, MENA, Africa, emerging markets and global, with positioning scatter, coverage heatmap, scoring, threat map, transaction and multiple comparables |
| `build/` | Generators: `python build.py model.xlsx`, then `python charts.py model_recalculated.xlsx charts/`, `python make_pricing_doc.py charts/ out.docx`, `python make_competitor_report.py charts/ out.docx` |

## Market focus
Egypt from month 1, Qatar month 4, Saudi Arabia month 10, UAE month 13, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman month 19. Self-serve sign-up from any country from day one. Price index: Egypt 60%, rest of MENA and Africa 70%, everywhere else list.

| EBITDA margin | n/m | (10%) | 30% | 38% | 48% |
| Employees, all Cairo based | 14 | 22 | 44 | 81 | 113 |

- **Pre-seed ask: USD 300k**, built from year-one cash cost of USD 301k plus a half-month buffer, less opening cash.
- **Monthly break even in month 20 (August 2028), inside year two**, and every month after is profitable. No further round is needed in the base case.
- **Marketing and community is the largest use of funds, at 36 per cent.** The rest goes to leadership and operations (28%), product and AI engineering (21%), sales and customer success (9%), and AI and cloud (6%).
- The team is lean and based in Cairo, paid in EGP. Qatar and the GCC are covered by founders and Cairo account executives travelling.
- Pre-money is USD 2.6m, a 20 per cent discount to the model's weighted fair value, so the round sells 10.3 per cent.
- Every check on the Summary sheet reads nil.

The opening position (section 2 of Assumption Setting) holds placeholders until the 31 December 2026 figures are confirmed.
