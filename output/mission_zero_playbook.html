01 / THE READWhat Techne actually is, and why it changes the plan

Techne is not a single conference you buy a booth at. It is a year-round machine with four moving parts, and each one is a different opportunity:

  • Techne Summit Cairo — 26–27 September 2026. The corporate and institutional end.
  • Techne Summit Alexandria — 3–5 October 2026. The big one: 60,000+ attendees last edition, 1,500 startups, 250 investors, 400 speakers, under the auspices of MCIT, ITIDA, DETGD and FEDCOC. This is where the youth are.
  • Techne Drifts — a year-round, one-day roadshow across 14 Egyptian cities, explicitly aimed at university students and youth entrepreneurs, structured as Egypt's first roadshow competition across the governorates. Its finale is hosted at Techne Summit Cairo.
  • Compete — Techne's competition track. 50+ competitions run to date, sector tournaments with partners like EdVentures, and in 2026 the Startup World Cup regional tournament in Alexandria on 4 October, whose winner goes to the San Francisco grand finale on 6 November for a USD 1,000,000 investment prize.
The thing to avoid

Running "a startup competition with EGP 50,000 for first place" at the same venue and the same week as a tournament advertising a USD 1,000,000 prize makes our prize the story, and our prize loses. And Techne already owns the national youth roadshow competition. A parallel youth competition is not a partnership — it is a competitor to their own asset, which is the fastest way to get a polite no.

Three decisions that follow from this

Decision 1 · positioning
Not a startup competition. A qualification challenge for people who do not have a startup yet.

Techne's Compete track serves teams with a prototype and traction. Ours serves the 19-year-old who has never spoken to a customer. Different entry point, different judging, no overlap — and it feeds their track next year instead of competing with it. Say this in the first line of the proposal.

Decision 2 · the event's role
Techne Summit is the launchpad, not the finish line.

There are 17 days to Cairo. A serious mission-completion challenge needs six weeks of founder work; three weeks of that would be recruitment. Announce and recruit on their stage, run the challenge afterwards, and bring the trophy back to a Techne moment — the Drifts finale is purpose-built for it.

Decision 3 · the prize
Cash is the smallest thing we are giving away — stop leading with it.

EGP 50,000 does not start a company. A verified evidence profile, a seat in a GrowthLabs programme, and a guaranteed slot in Techne's real Compete track next year are worth more to a 22-year-old and cost us less. Keep the cash; bury it third in the announcement.

02 / THE FORMATTwo tiers, one launch

The tension in this plan is that you want a demo day at the event, and the event is in 17 days. Both are satisfiable if the competition is split into a small on-site spectacle and a real national challenge behind it.

Tier 1 — the Sprint (the spectacle, on-site)

A small, fast, visible cohort that exists to produce a stage moment and prove the product works in front of an audience.

  • Recruited live at Cairo, 26–27 Sept, from a 45-minute "Mission 1 Live" workshop where every attendee completes Mission 1 on their phone in the room. Target 40 teams.
  • Missions 1–4 (problem analysis → problem statements) over the following week, with two online office hours.
  • Top 6 pitch for three minutes each on a stage in Alexandria, 5 Oct. Awards: certificates, mentor hours and Techne 2027 passes — no cash at this tier.
  • From that same stage, the National Challenge opens. The Sprint is the trailer.

Tier 2 — the National Challenge (the real one)

  • Opens 5 Oct, closes 16 Nov — six weeks.
  • Missions 1–8: from problem analysis through to a completed Business Model Canvas. This is the right scope — it is everything a founder can honestly do without a product, and it ends on an artefact a judge can read in two minutes.
  • Open nationally, promoted through Techne's 14 Drifts cities so it does not become a Cairo-and-Alexandria event.
  • Demo Day and the EGP 100,000 in late November, at a Techne Drift stop or a co-branded finale.
Why missions 1–8 and not all 15

Missions 9–15 require building a prototype, an MVP and a go-to-market. Asking students to do that in six weeks guarantees either fabricated answers or a field of twelve finishers. Missions 1–8 end at the Business Model Canvas, which is exactly the artefact a judging panel can compare across a hundred entries.

04 / JUDGINGThe part that decides whether this is credible

The moment cash is attached to an AI-scored submission, some entrants will write for the evaluator rather than talk to customers. This is the same failure the mission audit already flagged inside the product — Mission 14's self-rated product-market fit score is unfalsifiable — and prize money makes it worse. The judging has to be built assuming people will try.

  1. The AI score is a filter, never a verdict. It sorts the field and takes the top ~20% through. It does not pick winners and this is stated publicly, so nobody believes they are negotiating with a machine.
  2. Evidence artefacts, not prose. Every customer-interview claim carries a logged entry: interviewee first name, city, date, channel, and a consent checkbox. No log, no score on that question.
  3. Verification calls. For finalists only, we contact a random 10% of logged interviewees. Announce that this happens before entries open — the announcement does more work than the calls.
  4. Duplicate and template detection across submissions, because one popular YouTube tutorial produces forty identical problem statements.
  5. A blind human panel scores the shortlist without names or universities attached.
  6. The live pitch decides. Three minutes, four minutes of questions. Very little survives an unscripted question from a judge who has read the submission.

The published rubric

Publish this on day one. It doubles as a teaching tool, and it makes the result arguable only on the facts.

PillarWeightWhat earns the marks
Evidence35%Real conversations with real people, logged. Number, quality and how much the answers changed the founder's mind.
Problem clarity20%A specific person with a specific problem at a specific moment — not "young people in Egypt need..."
Mission completion depth20%All eight missions answered at depth, not eight one-line answers.
Business model coherence15%Does the canvas actually follow from the evidence, or was it written first?
Pitch and answers10%Finals only. Clarity under questioning.
Total100%AI-scored pillars are advisory; the panel's score is final.

08 / THE PARTNERSHIPWhat to ask Techne for, and what to give

Ask

  • Official track status and naming — the StartPad Youth Track at Techne Summit 2026, under the MEIW umbrella
  • A 45-minute workshop slot in Cairo (26 or 27 Sept)
  • A 30-minute stage slot in Alexandria on 5 Oct for the Sprint Final
  • Booth or activation space in both cities, comped or discounted against the programming we deliver
  • Agenda and event-app listing, two mailer mentions, three social posts
  • Two judges from their network, plus one from ITIDA or TIEC
  • Introductions into the 14 Drifts cities and their university contacts
  • First option to host the Demo Day at a Drift stop

Give

  • Fully produced youth programming they neither run nor pay for
  • EGP 100,000 in prizes, funded by us, awarded under joint branding
  • Techne co-branding on every certificate and on the track inside the platform — visibility that outlives the event
  • A scored, auditable pipeline they can reuse for Drifts and feed into Compete next year
  • Aggregated data on youth entrepreneurship by governorate — reportable to MCIT and ITIDA, which is what a summit under their auspices needs
  • Free StartPad licences for all Drifts participants for twelve months
  • A named feeder: our top 10 enter their Compete track in 2027
The pitch in one sentence

"You already run the national youth roadshow. We will give you the scoring engine behind it, pay for the prizes, and hand you the pipeline — for a stage slot and your logo next to ours."

Fallback ladder, if the answer is partly no

  1. No stage slot, booth only → run "Mission 1 Live" at the booth on the hour; keep everything else.
  2. No booth in time → buy passes, run it as an official side event or community event, which Techne offers explicitly.
  3. Nothing available this year → launch at the Drifts finale instead and sign a 2027 track agreement now, while their programming for next year is still open.

10 / RISKSWhat can actually go wrong

RiskHandling
Techne does not answer in time. Seventeen days out, their team is consumed by their own event.Send today, phone Friday, and treat Sunday 13 Sept as go/no-go. Have the fallback ladder pre-agreed internally so nobody has to make the call under pressure.
Entrants write for the AI.The whole of section 04. Announce verification before entries open.
Ten finishers instead of a hundred.Missions 1–8, not 15. Weekly clinics. Campus ambassadors. If week two is short, cut the requirement to Missions 1–6 publicly and early, never at judging time.
"They will steal my idea." A real and specific blocker with young Egyptian founders.Put "You own your idea. Always." on the landing page, in the rules, and on the stage. Nothing in the terms should assign IP or a right of first refusal — and if anyone suggests adding one, the competition is worth less than the clause.
Personal data of a thousand young people.Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151 of 2020) applies. Explicit consent at registration, a stated retention period, a named data owner, and no sharing of entrant contact details with sponsors.
Prize payment.Bank transfer against national ID and a signed receipt. Have an accountant confirm the tax treatment of prize money to individuals before announcing, not after winning.
A judge knows a finalist.Blind shortlist scoring, declared conflicts, and a published appeal window of 72 hours after results.

11 / NAMEMission Zero

The missions are the product, so the name should come from them. Mission Zero — المهمة صفر — is the mission before the missions: the decision to start. It works in both languages, it does not contain the word "competition", and it says the entry point is zero, which is precisely the audience.

Alternatives if it is taken: Zero to Evidence · First Mission (أول مهمة) · Proof (دليل).
