## 2. The intellectual position — "صرامة تحليلية بمعايير مؤسسية"

This is the firm's core argument and it should appear, in some form, in **every** proposal:

> نعمل على تقاطع الاستراتيجية والتمويل وعلوم البيانات. تُسنَد الحكم الاستراتيجي بالنمذجة الإحصائية
> ومحاكاة مونت كارلو والتحليل المالي المتقدّم. نستخدم الأدوات نفسها التي تعتمدها بنوك الاستثمار
> وشركات الملكية الخاصة — مُعايَرة على ظروف أسواق المنطقة. فتكون التوصية مُتحقَّقاً منها بالبيانات،
> لا سليمة منطقياً فحسب.

Three commercial consequences worth protecting in contracts and pricing:
1. **The deliverable is a defensible model, not an opinion.** Institutional tooling — Monte Carlo,
   DCF, real-options valuation — is the stated differentiator, so the engagement letter should
   describe deliverables in those terms and price them accordingly.
2. **Recommendations are data-verified.** That raises the bar on client-supplied inputs; make the
   input list contractual (see the proposal's *Client Responsibilities* section).
3. **Calibration to regional conditions** is the local moat against the global firms — say so
   explicitly when competing against Big-4 / international consultancies.

**The four-stage methodology — "عملية تسليم واحدة متصلة"**
`01 الطلب → 02 الجدوى → 03 التمويل → 04 التسعير`
*(Demand → Feasibility → Financing → Pricing.)* Each stage feeds the next, so "كل توصية مسنودة
بالتحليل الذي سبقها". **Commercially this is a staircase**: sell stage 01, and stages 02–04 are the
natural follow-on. Price each stage separately with a discount for the full sequence.

**Sectors (five asset classes):** متعدد الاستخدامات (mixed-use) · ضيافة (hospitality, incl. assets
near the Two Holy Mosques) · سكني (residential) · مكتبي (office) · تجاري (retail).

**Capital-raise process — "من أول اتصال إلى الإغلاق"**
`01 التقييم → 02 النمذجة → 03 الإعداد → 04 الجمع`
(Assessment → Modelling → Preparation → Raise.) Note stage 04 is *"إدارة التواصل مع المستثمرين
والتفاوض حتى إغلاق الجولة"* — this is the clause that most needs care in a contract; see
`05-annexes.md` §3 on success fees and regulatory licensing.

**Data rooms & due diligence — three products:**
1. **تجهيز غرفة البيانات** — full institutional data room: financial model, legal documents, growth
   metrics, coordinated investment narrative.
2. **الفحص النافي للجهالة** — independent review of an opportunity: quality of the numbers, growth
   assumptions, unit economics, market and execution risk. *Sold to the investor side.*
3. **التقرير الاستثماري** — investor-ready report that converts diligence into a clear decision,
   including reservations (نقاط التحفّظ) and proposed investment terms.

> **Note the two-sided business model.** Leap sells readiness to companies *and* diligence to
> investors. Keep a conflicts clause in every contract (see agreement §14) — the same deal must
> never be served from both sides without written disclosure and consent.

## 6. Brand & document system

Follow this when producing any client-facing document so proposals look like they came from the
same studio as the decks.

| Element | Spec observed in the decks |
|---|---|
| Page | 16:9 landscape, 720 × 405 pt |
| Covers & section dividers | Near-black ground, white type, faint geometric blocks |
| Content pages | Off-white ground (~#F4F4F4), near-black type, 1px hairline rules |
| Eyebrow | Uppercase Latin, wide letter-spacing — `W H Y   L E A P` — with a hairline and a 2-digit page number |
| Headline | Large light-weight Arabic display, right-aligned |
| Body | Short Arabic paragraph under the headline, max ~2 lines |
| Cards | Numbered `01`–`07` in a hairline grid, 2–3 per row, number top-corner, bold label, 2-line description |
| Stats | Oversized figure + small Arabic caption (`+50` / `نموذج مالي تم بناؤه ومراجعته`) |
| Language | Arabic-first RTL body; English only for section labels, project names and metrics |
| Contact block | Three columns: `PHONE · WHATSAPP` / `WEB · EMAIL` / `OFFICE` |

**Voice rules**
1. **Assert, then qualify.** "لا نقدّم المشورة فحسب — بل نبني الحل." Declarative first clause, em-dash, sharpening second clause.
2. **Numbers over adjectives.** "خمسة عشر سيناريو" beats "تحليل شامل".
3. **Name the decision, not the document.** Sell "قرار استثماري مسنود"، not "تقرير".
4. **Short sentences.** Deck body copy rarely exceeds two lines.
5. **Never oversell certainty.** The brand promise is *rigour*, which is exactly the promise a
   liability clause can survive; "نضمن العائد" is neither true nor insurable.

**Closing CTA used on every deck's last page:**
> **اطلب استشارة** — سواء كنت أمام قرار استثماري معقّد، أو تعمل على هيكلة تمويل مشروع، أو تخطّط
> لدخول سوق جديد — تحدّث إلينا في جلسة سريّة دون أي التزام.

## 7. What is *not* in the source material

Gaps to close before the first contract goes out. These are the fields left as `[...]` placeholders
in the templates:

- **Legal entity** — registered name, legal form, commercial register no., tax card no., VAT
  registration. Egypt-registered? Any KSA/UAE entity or a local partner for those markets?
- **Pricing** — no fee figures appear in any deck. See `02-service-catalog.md` §Fee basis for the
  build-up method; the rates themselves are yours to set.
- **Bank details** — for the payment clause (EGP and USD accounts, SWIFT/IBAN).
- **Licensing** — whether the firm holds, or needs, an FRA (Egypt) / CMA (KSA) / SCA (UAE)
  permission for the "جمع رأس المال" activity when it is remunerated by success fee. This one is
  material; see `05-annexes.md` §3.
- **Professional indemnity insurance** — existence and limit drive the liability cap you can defend.
- **Standard governing law** — Egyptian law + CRCICA arbitration is assumed in the templates; confirm.
