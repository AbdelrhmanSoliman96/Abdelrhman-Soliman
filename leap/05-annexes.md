# LEAP — Contract Annexes & Add-On Clauses

Short-form documents and drop-in clauses that attach to the main agreement
(`04-consulting-agreement-en.md` / `-ar.md`). Each is bilingual where it will be signed by a
regional client. Same warning applies: **drafting templates, not legal advice** — have counsel
review before first use.

| § | Document | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mutual NDA | Before any material disclosure — always precedes the proposal |
| 2 | Change Order | Any scope change after signature |
| 3 | Success Fee Addendum | Capital raising, financing, partnerships, exits |
| 4 | Retainer Addendum | Continuing mandates — IR, advisory, interim management |
| 5 | Third-Party Reliance Letter | A lender or investor asks to rely on a study |
| 6 | Short-Form Engagement Letter | Small assignments where a full agreement is disproportionate |

---

## 1 · Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — اتفاقية عدم إفصاح متبادلة

**Between** `[LEAP entity]` and `[Counterparty]`, dated `[date]`.

1. **Purpose.** Evaluating and potentially entering into an advisory engagement concerning
   `[subject]` (the "Purpose").
2. **Confidential Information.** All non-public information disclosed by either party in any form,
   including financial data, models, business plans, pricing, client and investor identities,
   drawings, and the fact and content of discussions between the parties.
3. **Obligations.** Each party shall use the other's Confidential Information solely for the
   Purpose; keep it confidential; disclose it only to personnel, affiliates and professional
   advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by equivalent duties; and protect it with no
   less care than it applies to its own confidential information.
4. **Exclusions.** Information that is or becomes public without breach; was lawfully known before
   disclosure; is received from a third party without restriction; or is independently developed.
5. **Compelled disclosure.** Permitted where required by law, regulation or court order, with prior
   notice to the disclosing party where lawful, and limited to what is required.
6. **No licence, no obligation.** Nothing grants any intellectual property right, or obliges either
   party to proceed with a transaction or engagement.
7. **Return or destruction.** On written request, each party shall return or destroy the other's
   Confidential Information, save for one archival copy retained for compliance and for copies held
   in routine electronic backups, which remain subject to this agreement.
8. **Term.** This agreement runs for `[2]` years from its date; the confidentiality obligations
   survive for `[3]` years from disclosure, and indefinitely for trade secrets.
9. **No solicitation of personnel** for `[12]` months from the date of this agreement.
10. **Remedies.** Damages may be inadequate; each party may seek injunctive relief without proof of
    actual damage.
11. **Governing law.** `[Egyptian law; CRCICA arbitration, seated in Cairo, in Arabic]`.

**النص العربي المختصر.** يلتزم كل طرف بالحفاظ على سرّية المعلومات التي يتلقاها من الطرف الآخر،
واستخدامها حصراً في غرض تقييم التعاون المحتمل بشأن `[الموضوع]`، وعدم إفشائها لأي غير إلا لمن يلزمه
الاطّلاع من العاملين والمستشارين وبشرط التزامهم بذات التعهد. ولا يسري الالتزام على المعلومة العامة
أو المعلومة سلفاً بطريق مشروع أو المطوَّرة استقلالاً أو الواجب إفشاؤها قانوناً. ولا تُنشئ هذه
الاتفاقية أي ترخيص بحق ملكية فكرية ولا التزاماً بإتمام أي تعاقد. مدة الاتفاقية `[سنتان]`، ويستمر
التزام السرّية `[3]` سنوات من تاريخ الإفصاح ودائماً بالنسبة للأسرار التجارية. ويلتزم الطرفان بعدم
استقطاب العاملين لدى الطرف الآخر لمدة `[12]` شهراً. ويخضع الاتفاق لقوانين `[جمهورية مصر العربية]`،
ويُحسم أي نزاع بالتحكيم وفق قواعد `[CRCICA]` بالقاهرة وباللغة العربية.

## 3 · Success Fee Addendum — ملحق أتعاب النجاح

> **Check licensing before you use this.** Charging a success fee for arranging finance or
> introducing investors can be a **regulated activity**: Egypt (FRA), Saudi Arabia (CMA), UAE
> (SCA / DFSA / FSRA). Confirm the firm's position in each market where it raises capital for a
> fee. Where the position is unresolved, sell the *preparation* work (data room, model, research)
> on a fixed fee and do not take a success fee on the raise. This is the single highest-risk
> commercial term in the whole template set.

**3.1 Definitions**
- **"Transaction"** — `[the completion of an equity investment in / debt facility to / sale of]`
  `[the Client / the Project]`.
- **"Transaction Value"** — the gross amount of funds **actually received** by the Client in
  cleared funds, `[including / excluding]` amounts committed but not drawn, `[including /
  excluding]` amounts from Excluded Parties.
- **"Excluded Parties"** — the counterparties listed in Appendix 1, being parties already in
  advanced discussion with the Client at the date of this Addendum; no success fee is payable on
  funds received from them `[/ a reduced fee of [x]% applies]`.

**3.2 Success Fee**
A success fee of **`[x]`%** of Transaction Value, payable on completion.
*Optional tiered (declining) scale — set the bands to the deal size you actually target:*

| Tranche of Transaction Value | Rate |
|---|---|
| First `[amount]` | `[x]`% |
| Next `[amount]` | `[x]`% |
| Above `[amount]` | `[x]`% |

**3.3 Interaction with the work fee**
The monthly work fee under Schedule B `[IS / IS NOT]` credited against the success fee.
*Say which — this is the most common cause of dispute at closing.*

**3.4 Payment**
The success fee falls due on the date the Client receives the funds, and is payable within `[5]`
Working Days. Where funds are received in tranches, the fee is payable pro rata on each tranche
within `[5]` Working Days of receipt.

**3.5 Tail period**
If, within `[12]` months of termination or expiry, the Client completes a Transaction with any
party that the Consultant introduced or materially engaged with during the mandate (as evidenced by
the outreach tracker delivered with the monthly report), the success fee remains payable.

**3.6 Exclusivity**
For the term, the Consultant is `[the Client's exclusive adviser / one of the Client's advisers]`
for the Transaction. `[Where exclusive: the Client shall route all investor approaches through the
Consultant during the term.]`

**3.7 Non-circumvention**
The Client shall not, directly or through an affiliate, circumvent the Consultant in dealings with
introduced parties for the purpose of avoiding the success fee.

**3.8 No guarantee**
Nothing in this Addendum obliges the Consultant to procure funding, or guarantees that a
Transaction will occur or on what terms. The Consultant's obligation is one of means, not result
(clause 9.3 of the Agreement).

**صيغة عربية للبنود الجوهرية.**
تُستحق **أتعاب نجاح بنسبة `[x]`%** من قيمة الصفقة، وتُحتسب قيمة الصفقة على أساس المبالغ **المحصَّلة
فعلاً** في حساب العميل. وتُستحق الأتعاب في تاريخ استلام العميل للأموال وتُسدَّد خلال `[5]` أيام
عمل، وبالتناسب عند الاستلام على شرائح. أتعاب المتابعة الشهرية `[تُخصم / لا تُخصم]` من أتعاب النجاح.
وتظل أتعاب النجاح مستحقة إذا أتمّ العميل الصفقة خلال `[12]` شهراً من انتهاء التكليف مع أي طرف عرّفه
به الاستشاري أو تواصل معه جوهرياً خلال المدة، وفقاً لسجل التواصل المرفق بالتقارير الشهرية. ولا
يلتزم الاستشاري بضمان الحصول على التمويل أو إتمام الصفقة؛ فالتزامه بذل عناية لا تحقيق غاية.

## 4 · Retainer Addendum — ملحق أتعاب المتابعة الشهرية

For continuing mandates: investor relations (BD-04), standing advisory (SF-01), interim management
(SF-06), BI maintenance (SF-03).

**4.1 Committed capacity.** The Consultant commits `[n]` person-days per calendar month, allocated
as `[Principal: n days / Senior Analyst: n days]`.
**4.2 Fee.** `[amount]` `[currency]` per month, invoiced **in advance** on the `[1st]` of each month,
payable within `[15]` days.
**4.3 Minimum term.** `[3]` months, then rolling monthly.
**4.4 Notice.** `[30]` days' written notice to terminate after the minimum term.
**4.5 Carry-over.** Unused days `[may be carried forward one month only / do not carry forward]`.
Days used above the commitment are charged at the Schedule C day rates.
**4.6 Scope.** The mandate covers `[list]`. Anything else is a change order under clause 8.
**4.7 Reporting.** A written monthly report of work performed and days used, issued by the `[5th]`
of the following month.
**4.8 Interim placements.** Where a named individual is placed under SF-06, clause 17
(non-solicitation) applies with particular force, and the individual reports to `[name, title]`
while remaining an employee or contractor of the Consultant at all times.

**صيغة عربية.** يلتزم الاستشاري بتخصيص `[...]` يوم عمل شهرياً مقابل أتعاب قدرها `[...]` تُفوتَر
مقدماً في `[الأول]` من كل شهر وتُسدَّد خلال `[15]` يوماً. الحد الأدنى للمدة `[3]` أشهر، ثم تتجدد
شهرياً، وينتهي التكليف بإخطار كتابي مدته `[30]` يوماً. الأيام غير المستخدمة `[تُرحَّل شهراً واحداً
فقط / لا تُرحَّل]`، وما زاد على الالتزام يُحتسب بالأسعار اليومية في الملحق (ج). ويقدّم الاستشاري

## 5 · Third-Party Reliance Letter — خطاب اعتماد لطرف ثالث

Issued when a lender, investor or buyer asks to rely on a study Leap prepared for a client. Never
let a deliverable travel to a third party without one.

> `[Date]`
> To: `[Third party legal name and address]`
> Re: `[Title of the report]` dated `[date]`, prepared for `[Client]` (the "Report")
>
> 1. We consent to `[Third Party]` receiving and relying on the Report **solely** for the purpose
>    of `[evaluating the provision of a credit facility to the Client in respect of the Project]`,
>    and for no other purpose.
> 2. The Report was prepared on the basis of information available, and market conditions
>    prevailing, at its date. We have not updated it and are under no obligation to do so.
> 3. The Report is decision-support. It is not a valuation, an audit, or a guarantee of any
>    outcome, and it contains projections that are estimates based on stated assumptions.
> 4. Our total aggregate liability to `[Third Party]` in connection with the Report, however
>    arising, shall not exceed `[amount]` `[/ the fees received for the Report]`, and we exclude
>    liability for indirect or consequential loss. Nothing limits liability for fraud.
> 5. `[Third Party]` shall keep the Report confidential and shall not disclose it further without
>    our prior written consent.
> 6. This letter is governed by `[Egyptian law]`, and `[Third Party]`'s acceptance of the Report
>    constitutes acceptance of these terms.
>
> `[LEAP entity]` — `[Name, Title, Signature]`

## 6 · Short-Form Engagement Letter — خطاب تكليف مختصر

For assignments under `[amount]` where a full agreement is disproportionate. Keep it to one page.
It must still carry the four clauses that actually protect the firm: **no guarantee**, **reliance
limits**, **liability cap**, **IP on payment**.

> **`[Date]` · Ref `LEAP-[YYYY]-[###]`**
> **To:** `[Client]`, attn `[name]`
> **Subject:** Engagement — `[assignment]`
>
> Thank you for asking us to assist with `[assignment]`. This letter sets out our engagement.
>
> **1. Scope.** `[2–4 lines, or reference to the attached proposal]`
> **2. Deliverables.** `[list]`, delivered by `[date]` / within `[n]` working days of receipt of
> `[inputs]` and the mobilisation payment.
> **3. Fees.** `[amount]` `[currency]`, `[excluding]` VAT — `[50]`% on acceptance (non-refundable),
> `[50]`% on delivery. Payable within `[15]` days of invoice. Out-of-scope work at `[day rate]`.
> Two consolidated revision rounds included.
> **4. Your inputs.** `[list]`, required by `[date]`. Late inputs extend delivery day for day.
> **5. Basis of our work.** Our work is decision-support based on information you provide and
> market conditions at the date of delivery. We do not audit that information, and we do not
> guarantee any return, financing, valuation or transaction outcome.
> **6. Reliance.** The deliverables are for your internal use and for the purpose above. No third
> party may rely on them without our written consent.
> **7. Intellectual property.** Our methodologies, models and templates remain ours. You receive a
> licence to use the deliverables internally once fees are paid in full.
> **8. Confidentiality.** Both of us will keep the other's information confidential.
> **9. Liability.** Our total liability is limited to the fees paid under this engagement, save for
> fraud or wilful misconduct. We exclude indirect and consequential loss.
> **10. Law.** `[Egyptian law; disputes to CRCICA arbitration in Cairo]`.
>
> Please confirm by signing below or replying to this email with your acceptance.
>
> `[LEAP entity]` — `[Name, Title]` · Accepted for `[Client]`: `[Name, Title, Date]`

**صيغة عربية مختصرة للفقرات الحاكمة.** يُنفَّذ التكليف على أساس المعلومات المقدَّمة من العميل
وظروف السوق في تاريخ التسليم، دون تدقيق مستقل لتلك المعلومات، ودون أي ضمان لتحقيق عائد أو الحصول
على تمويل أو إتمام صفقة. وتُعدّ المخرجات للاستخدام الداخلي للعميل وللغرض المذكور وحده، ولا يجوز
اعتماد الغير عليها دون موافقة كتابية. وتظل منهجيات الاستشاري ونماذجه وقوالبه ملكاً له، ويمنح العميل
ترخيصاً باستخدام المخرجات داخلياً عند سداد الأتعاب كاملة. ولا تتجاوز المسؤولية الإجمالية للاستشاري
قيمة الأتعاب المسدَّدة، فيما عدا الغش والخطأ العمدي. ويخضع التكليف لقوانين `[جمهورية مصر العربية]`.
