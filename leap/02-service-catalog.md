# LEAP — Service Catalog (Proposal Building Blocks)

Every service Leap advertises in the 2026 decks, rewritten as a **paste-able scope block**:
what you do, what the client gets, what you need from them, how long it takes, and how to price it.

**How to use.** Pick the blocks the engagement needs → paste into `03-proposal-template-ar.md`
§3 and §4 → paste the same blocks verbatim into **Schedule A (نطاق العمل)** of the agreement.
Proposal scope and contract scope must be word-identical; that is what stops scope disputes.

**Durations** are working-day estimates from the day the *complete* input pack is received —
never from signature. **Effort** is person-days, the input to the fee build-up in §F.

## A. Real Estate Advisory — الاستشارات العقارية

Sold as a connected staircase: `الطلب → الجدوى → التمويل → التسعير`. Quote stages individually,
then quote the bundle at a discount (10–15% is the usual market gesture) with one mobilisation fee.

### RE-01 · دراسة السوق وتحليل الطلب — Market Study & Demand Analysis
**Scope.** Macro and sector scan for the subject catchment; supply pipeline census (delivered,
under construction, announced); absorption and take-up history; pricing and rent benchmarks for
comparable assets; demand drivers (demographics, income bands, employment nodes, infrastructure);
segmentation of the addressable buyer/tenant pool; demand–supply gap by product type.
**Deliverables.** (1) Market study report, Arabic or English, ~40–60 pages; (2) comparables
database (Excel) — asset, developer, price/m², unit mix, status, absorption; (3) demand-gap matrix
by product type; (4) one presentation to the client's board or investment committee.
**Client inputs.** Site title/coordinates and area schedule; any existing surveys or brokerage
data; access to the developer's own sales history if an operating asset.
**Duration.** 15–20 working days. **Effort.** 12–18 person-days.
**Fee basis.** Fixed fee. Add a travel/field-survey line if primary fieldwork is required.

### RE-02 · دراسة أفضل استخدام للأرض — Highest & Best Use
**Scope.** Test legally permissible, physically possible, financially feasible and maximally
productive uses for the plot. Regulatory envelope (zoning, FAR/GFA, height, setbacks, parking);
three to five development scenarios; concept massing assumptions per scenario; comparative
financial screen (revenue, cost, margin, IRR) per scenario; ranked recommendation with rationale.
**Deliverables.** (1) HBU report with scenario matrix; (2) scenario screening model (Excel);
(3) recommended development concept with indicative area programme.
**Client inputs.** Title deed, plot survey, approved zoning/building conditions, any master-plan
constraints, land cost or book value.
**Duration.** 12–18 working days. **Effort.** 10–15 person-days.
**Fee basis.** Fixed fee; commonly bundled with RE-01.

### RE-03 · دراسة الجدوى — Feasibility Study
**Scope.** Full technical-commercial feasibility on the selected concept: area programme and
efficiency ratios; construction cost build-up (hard, soft, contingency) by phase; revenue model by
product line; sales/lease-up schedule; project cash flow across the development period; NPV, IRR,
equity multiple, payback, peak funding requirement; sensitivity on the material variables; risk
register with mitigations.
**Deliverables.** (1) Feasibility study report; (2) the full financial model (RE-04) as the
computational backbone; (3) executive summary deck for lenders/investors; (4) risk register.
**Client inputs.** Approved concept/masterplan or architectural brief; cost estimates or BOQ if
available; target programme dates; land cost and payment terms; expected financing structure.
**Duration.** 20–30 working days. **Effort.** 20–30 person-days.
**Fee basis.** Fixed fee, or fixed fee tiered by GFA/project value.
**Guard-rail.** State in writing that the study is a decision-support document based on stated
assumptions and is **not** a valuation report, an audit, or a guarantee of outcome — and that it is
addressed to the client alone (see agreement §12, third-party reliance).

### RE-04 · النمذجة المالية للمشاريع العقارية — Real Estate Financial Modelling
**Scope.** Institutional-grade Excel model: monthly or quarterly development cash flow; cost
S-curve; sales/collection curve with instalment plan mechanics; debt module (drawdown, interest
during construction, amortisation, DSCR covenants); equity waterfall and promote structure if
required; scenario manager; **Monte Carlo simulation** on the driving variables; output dashboard
(IRR, NPV, MOIC, peak equity, DSCR, break-even).
**Deliverables.** (1) Unlocked, documented Excel model with an assumptions sheet and a change log;
(2) model manual / walkthrough note; (3) one handover training session; (4) outputs dashboard.
**Client inputs.** Confirmed area programme, cost plan, pricing assumptions, financing term sheet.
**Duration.** 15–25 working days. **Effort.** 15–25 person-days.
**Fee basis.** Fixed fee. Price model *rebuilds* and model *reviews* separately — a review of a
third-party model is 4–8 person-days and carries its own findings memo.

### RE-05 · استراتيجية التمويل وهيكلة رأس المال — Financing Strategy & Capital Structuring
**Scope.** Funding requirement and drawdown profile; optimal mix across senior debt, mezzanine,
developer equity and off-plan pre-sales; WACC optimisation across structures; lender/investor
target list by appetite; indicative term-sheet parameters; preparation of the financing pack;
support through lender Q&A to financial close.
**Deliverables.** (1) Financing strategy memorandum with recommended structure; (2) capital
structure model with WACC comparison; (3) lender/investor target list; (4) financing information
pack; (5) support through close (defined number of meetings).
**Client inputs.** Audited financials or management accounts; existing facility documents;
shareholder structure; security available.
**Duration.** 20–30 working days to pack-ready; close is market-dependent.
**Effort.** 18–25 person-days + close support. **Fee basis.** Retainer + success fee — see §F
and the success-fee addendum. **Never promise a close date.**

### RE-06 · برمجة المساحات وتحسين مزيج المنتج — Space Programming & Product-Mix Optimisation
**Scope.** Translate demand evidence into a unit mix: typologies, areas, counts, floor stacking,
efficiency (NSA/GFA), amenity allocation, phasing. Optimise mix against price/m² by typology,
absorption capacity and margin — the exact technique behind proof case 02 (fifteen scenarios,
shift toward villas).
**Deliverables.** (1) Recommended area programme and unit schedule; (2) mix-optimisation model
with the scenario set; (3) phasing plan tied to absorption.
**Client inputs.** Architectural drawings or massing, buildable area confirmation, cost per m² by typology.
**Duration.** 10–15 working days. **Effort.** 8–14 person-days.
**Fee basis.** Fixed fee; strong upsell after RE-01/RE-03.

### RE-07 · استراتيجية التسعير — Pricing Strategy
**Scope.** Price list by typology, floor, view and orientation; premium/discount ladder;
instalment plan design and its discounted-cash-flow cost; launch phasing and price-escalation
calendar; competitor price-position map; sensitivity of absorption to price.
**Deliverables.** (1) Full price list (Excel); (2) payment-plan matrix with NPV per plan;
(3) launch and escalation calendar; (4) pricing strategy note for the sales team.
**Client inputs.** Final unit schedule, cost base, target IRR and absorption period, competitor prices.
**Duration.** 10–15 working days. **Effort.** 8–12 person-days.
**Fee basis.** Fixed fee, or fixed + performance bonus on absorption achieved in the first N months.

## B. Strategic & Financial Advisory — الاستشارات الاستراتيجية والمالية

### SF-01 · الاستشارات الاستراتيجية والاستثمارية — Strategic & Investment Advisory
**Scope.** Board-level advisory on the decisions that move the needle: market and competitive
intelligence, strategic options framing, investment screening criteria, portfolio prioritisation,
capital allocation logic.
**Deliverables.** Strategy memorandum; options matrix with a scored recommendation; board pack;
implementation roadmap with owners and milestones.
**Duration.** 4–8 weeks. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee per phase, or monthly retainer for standing advisory.

### SF-02 · النمذجة والتحليل المالي — Financial Modelling & Analysis
**Scope.** Three-statement operating model; driver-based forecasting; unit economics; scenario and
sensitivity architecture; valuation (DCF, comparables, precedent transactions, real options where
optionality is material).
**Deliverables.** Documented model; valuation memo with a football-field range; assumptions log;
handover session.
**Duration.** 3–5 weeks. **Effort.** 15–25 person-days. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee.

### SF-03 · تحليلات البيانات وذكاء الأعمال — Data Analytics & BI
**Scope.** Convert raw operational data into decision assets: data audit and cleaning, KPI
definition, dashboard build, predictive/forecast models, adoption support.
**Deliverables.** KPI dictionary; live dashboard; forecasting model; user training.
**Duration.** 4–8 weeks. **Fee basis.** Fixed build fee + optional monthly maintenance retainer.
**Watch the IP clause** — tooling and code here are firm IP, licensed to the client (agreement §11).

### SF-04 · إدارة المخاطر والمحاكاة — Risk Management & Simulation
**Scope.** Risk identification and register; quantification of uncertainty; **Monte Carlo**
simulation of the model's key variables; stress testing of assumptions; resilience and mitigation
framework; probability-weighted outcome distributions rather than single-point answers.
**Deliverables.** Risk register with owners; simulation model; outcome distribution report
(P10/P50/P90); mitigation plan.
**Duration.** 2–4 weeks. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee; often an add-on module to SF-02 or RE-04.

### SF-05 · تحسين التدفقات النقدية — Cash-Flow Optimisation
**Scope.** Diagnose liquidity bottlenecks; working-capital cycle analysis (DSO, DPO, DIO); release
trapped working capital; 13-week cash-flow forecasting; collection and payment policy redesign;
sustainable liquidity strategy.
**Deliverables.** Cash diagnostic report quantifying the release opportunity; 13-week rolling
cash-flow tool; policy recommendations with an implementation timetable.
**Duration.** 3–6 weeks. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee, or fixed + success fee on cash actually released
(defined precisely — measurement disputes live here).

### SF-06 · الكفاءات والإدارة المؤقتة — Talent & Interim Management
**Scope.** Placement of fractional or interim finance leadership and specialists — CFOs,
financial controllers, data scientists, sector advisors — for defined mission-critical mandates.
**Deliverables.** Named professional, agreed days per month, defined mandate and reporting line,
monthly progress report.
**Duration.** Rolling monthly, 3-month minimum term.
**Fee basis.** **Monthly retainer** per placed professional (day rate × committed days), invoiced
monthly in advance. Two clauses matter: a notice period (30 days) and **non-solicitation** — the
client must not hire the individual directly during the term and for 12 months after (agreement §17).

## C. Business Development & Market Entry — تطوير الأعمال ودخول الأسواق

### BD-01 · دخول السوق — Market Entry
**Scope.** Market attractiveness assessment; regulatory and licensing requirements; entry-mode
options (branch, subsidiary, JV, distributor, franchise) with a comparative matrix; localisation
requirements; cost-to-enter model; 12-month entry roadmap.
**Deliverables.** Market entry study; entry-mode recommendation; regulatory checklist; cost-to-enter
model; phased roadmap.
**Duration.** 4–6 weeks per geography. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee per market.
**Exclude legal work explicitly** — you advise on requirements; licensed counsel files.

### BD-02 · تيسير الشراكات — Partnership Facilitation
**Scope.** Partner profile definition; long list → screened short list; discreet approach and
introduction; commercial framework design; negotiation support to heads of terms.
**Deliverables.** Partner criteria note; screened short list with profiles; introductions;
term-sheet/HoT support.
**Duration.** 6–12 weeks. **Fee basis.** Retainer + success fee on a signed definitive agreement.

### BD-03 · استراتيجيات النمو — Growth Strategies
**Scope.** Growth plan grounded in unit economics and demonstrated market absorption capacity:
channel economics, pricing, capacity and hiring plan, capital requirement, KPI tree.
**Deliverables.** Growth strategy document; unit-economics model; KPI dashboard; quarterly milestones.
**Duration.** 3–6 weeks. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee, optionally + quarterly advisory retainer.

### BD-04 · علاقات المستثمرين — Investor Relations
**Scope.** Post-close investor reporting discipline: reporting calendar and templates, quarterly
investor update pack, KPI reporting, coordinated investment narrative, management of investor Q&A.
**Deliverables.** IR calendar; reporting templates; quarterly update pack; narrative document.
**Duration.** Ongoing. **Fee basis.** **Monthly or quarterly retainer** — the firm's best recurring
revenue line and the natural annuity after every closed raise.

## D. Investment & Capital — الاستثمار والتمويل

### IC-01 · غرف بيانات المستثمرين — Investor Data Rooms
**Scope.** Build an institutional data room that survives diligence: index architecture; financial
model and historicals; corporate and legal documents; commercial contracts; growth metrics pack;
cap table; coordinated investment narrative; access permissions and Q&A tracking.
**Deliverables.** Structured, permissioned data room; document index and gap list; investment
narrative; Q&A log.
**Client inputs.** Corporate documents, financials, contracts, cap table, KPI history.
**Duration.** 3–5 weeks. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee.

### IC-02 · النمذجة والتقييم — Modelling & Valuation
**Scope.** Investor-grade model and valuation that withstand scrutiny: revenue build, cost
structure, headcount plan, working capital, cap-table and dilution mechanics across rounds,
valuation range with methodology justification.
**Deliverables.** Financial model; valuation memorandum; cap table with round scenarios.
**Duration.** 3–4 weeks. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee.

### IC-03 · الفحص النافي للجهالة — Due Diligence *(sold to the investor side)*
**Scope.** Independent examination of an opportunity: quality of the numbers, growth assumptions,
unit economics, market risk, execution risk, management assessment, deal-structure commentary.
**Deliverables.** Due diligence report; red-flag register; **investment report** converting the
findings into a clear decision, including reservations (نقاط التحفّظ) and proposed investment terms.
**Duration.** 3–6 weeks depending on target size. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee.
**Conflicts.** Never run IC-03 on a target where Leap holds an IC-01/IC-05 mandate on the other
side without written disclosure and consent from both.

### IC-04 · بحوث السوق — Market Research
**Scope.** Market sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM) with stated methodology; competitive benchmarking; deep
sector studies regionally and globally; pricing and channel intelligence.
**Deliverables.** Research report; sizing model with sources; competitor matrix.
**Duration.** 2–4 weeks. **Fee basis.** Fixed fee.

### IC-05 · استراتيجية جمع رأس المال — Capital Raising Strategy
**Scope.** The four-stage path: **التقييم** (understand the business, market and numbers) →
**النمذجة** (model, valuation and scenarios that survive diligence) → **الإعداد** (data room, pitch
deck, investment narrative) → **الجمع** (manage investor outreach and negotiation to close).
Investor target list by thesis, stage and cheque size; outreach sequencing; meeting preparation;
term-sheet commentary.
**Deliverables.** Raise roadmap; investor target list; pitch deck; data room (IC-01); model
(IC-02); outreach tracker; weekly progress reports through the raise.
**Duration.** Preparation 4–6 weeks; the raise itself is market-dependent — **never contract to a
closing date**.
**Fee basis.** Monthly retainer + success fee on funds actually received. See `05-annexes.md` §3
**and confirm the licensing position first** — remunerated capital-raising can be a regulated
activity (FRA in Egypt, CMA in KSA, SCA in UAE). Where licensing is unresolved, sell the
*preparation* (IC-01/02/04) on a fixed fee and keep introductions unremunerated by success fee.

## E. Standard deliverable vocabulary

Use these exact nouns; they are countable, and countable deliverables end arguments.

| Deliverable | Contractual definition to use |
|---|---|
| تقرير / Report | PDF, Arabic or English as specified, delivered by email to the named recipients |
| نموذج مالي / Financial model | Excel workbook, unlocked, with assumptions sheet, change log and documentation tab |
| عرض تقديمي / Presentation | Slide deck (PDF + source), plus one presentation session of up to 90 minutes |
| غرفة بيانات / Data room | Structured, permissioned folder tree with an index; hosting platform named |
| قائمة مستهدفين / Target list | Excel: entity, contact, thesis fit, cheque size, status |
| جلسة / Session | Up to 90 minutes, online or in person, with written follow-up notes |
| مراجعة / Revision round | One consolidated set of client comments, returned within 5 working days |

**Always cap revisions.** Two rounds included; further rounds at the daily rate. Unlimited revisions
are how a fixed-fee engagement turns into a loss.

## F. Fee basis — how to price

No fee figures appear anywhere in the three decks, so nothing here is "the Leap rate". This is the
build-up to produce one.

**Step 1 — Effort.** From the blocks above, total the person-days by grade:
Principal / Senior Analyst / Analyst.

**Step 2 — Blended rate.** Set a target day rate per grade covering cost, utilisation (assume 60%
billable) and margin. Fill in and keep in a private file, never in the proposal:

| Grade | Day rate | Typical share of an engagement |
|---|---|---|
| Principal (Abdelrhman / Ahmed) | `[...]` | 20–30% |
| Senior Analyst | `[...]` | 40–50% |
| Analyst | `[...]` | 30–40% |

**Step 3 — Pick the model.**

| Model | Use when | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed fee** | Scope is definable and bounded — most studies and models | Quote per phase; invoice against milestones |
| **Retainer** | Continuing mandate — interim management (SF-06), IR (BD-04), advisory | Monthly, in advance, defined days/month, 3-month minimum |
| **Retainer + success fee** | Capital raising (IC-05, RE-05), partnerships (BD-02) | Monthly work fee **credited or not credited** against the success fee — say which |
| **Fixed + performance bonus** | Pricing (RE-07), cash release (SF-05) | Base fee + bonus on a precisely defined, measurable outcome |
| **Day rate** | Out-of-scope requests, extra revision rounds, expert sessions | Published in the proposal so change orders need no renegotiation |

**Step 4 — Payment schedule.** Default for fixed-fee work:
**40% mobilisation (non-refundable) · 30% at interim deliverable · 30% on final delivery.**
For engagements over ~12 weeks, switch to monthly invoicing against a milestone plan.
Work starts on receipt of the mobilisation payment, not on signature — put that in both documents.

**Step 5 — Add the commercial lines.** VAT/tax treatment; withholding tax gross-up; out-of-pocket
expenses (travel, data subscriptions, field survey) charged at cost with prior written approval and
a cap; currency of invoice and, for non-EGP work, the FX reference; validity of the offer (30 days).

**Discount discipline.** Discount the *bundle*, never the day rate. Bundling RE-01→RE-07 at 10–15%
off the sum of stages protects the rate card and sells the staircase in one signature.
