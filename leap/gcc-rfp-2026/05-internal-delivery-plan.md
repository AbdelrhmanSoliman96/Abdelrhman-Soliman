## 8. AI governance — standing operating procedure

We have committed to §1.4 D of the RFP in writing. This is how we keep that commitment true.

1. **Named reviewer per engagement.** Recorded in the engagement tracker at scoping. For any
   quantitative output it is the Principal or the Senior Advisor — no exceptions.
2. **Enterprise configurations only**, with training on inputs disabled and retention controlled. No
   consumer accounts touch client work.
3. **Hard prohibition on sensitive data** in any uncontrolled tool: innovator financials, cap tables,
   beneficiary or patient data, unpublished evidence, anything commercially sensitive.
4. **AI use log per engagement** — tool, purpose, what it was applied to, reviewer, date. Disclosable
   to GCC on request, as we have undertaken.
5. **Never for findings.** AI does not set assumptions, select recommendations, determine valuations
   or generate figures presented as analysis.
6. **Associate briefing.** Every associate is briefed on this SOP and GCC's AI policy before starting.
7. **Opt-out respected.** Where GCC or an innovator prefers no AI involvement, we deliver without it
   and record that in the scoping note.
8. **Adopt GCC's policy on award** — it is shared at contract negotiation. Review ours against it in
   November 2026 and reconcile any difference in GCC's favour.
