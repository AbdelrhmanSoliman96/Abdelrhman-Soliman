**We are strongest in Egypt and the wider Arabic-speaking region** — where our presence is physical
rather than remote, our network converts into introductions that actually happen, and we work in the
innovator's own language. For GCC's portfolio in North Africa and the Middle East we are a local
provider, not a global firm flying in. **Outside MENA we deliver remotely and deliberately:**
Categories 1, 2 and 5 are analytical and facilitative and travel well to innovators anywhere with
reliable connectivity. We do not claim in-country presence we do not have, and for Category 4 we
accept engagements only in markets where we hold real standing or a named local associate.

## Delivery model

**Right-sized to stage.** High-touch where the decision is consequential and the innovator is close
to scale; low-touch where a focused diagnostic or group session does the job. We propose the lighter
option when it is sufficient, and say so.

**Time-bound and decision-centric.** Every engagement is scoped against one named decision, with a
duration agreed in advance, countable outputs and an end date. We do not run open-ended
capacity-strengthening.

| Stage | What happens | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| **Scoping call** | With GCC and the innovator: the decision at stake, the barrier, what "resolved" looks like | Within 5 business days of assignment |
| **Scoping note** | One page: objective, activities, outputs, hours, dates, what we need from the innovator | 3 business days after the call |
| **Delivery** | Working sessions on a fixed cadence, in the innovator's time zone, in Arabic or English | Typically 4–10 weeks |
| **Handover** | Walkthrough so the innovator can operate the outputs unaided | Final week |
| **Close-out note** | To GCC: what changed, what was decided, what we would watch, hours against scope | Within 5 business days of close |

**Languages.** Arabic and English at native or fluent standard across the core team. `[[Add French
or Spanish only with a named associate who delivers in them.]]`

**Time zones and access.** Cairo (UTC+2/+3) overlaps a full working day with Africa, the Middle East,
Europe and South Asia, and mornings in the Americas; we schedule to the innovator's hours. Sessions

**Travel.** Not proposed; our fees are built for remote delivery. Where GCC and an innovator judge an

# 4. Our Approach to Using AI Responsibly
*(RFP §3.1 item 4 — 1 page; addresses §1.4 D)*

We use AI tools as an accelerant inside a controlled process. They never decide anything, and they
never sit between us and responsibility for our work. Our commitments map directly to §1.4 D:

**(a) Human review before anything leaves our hands.** No AI-generated output reaches GCC, its
representatives or an innovator until a named, qualified team member has reviewed and approved it.
For financial models, valuations and any quantitative output the reviewer is the Engagement Principal
or Senior Advisor — the same person who would sign the work if no AI had touched it. Review is logged
against the reviewer's name.

**(b) Decisions stay with people.** AI is used for drafting, structuring, summarising, market
scanning, code assistance in model construction, and checking our own reasoning for gaps. It is never
used to select a recommendation, set an assumption, determine a valuation, or decide what an
innovator should do.

**(c) Full responsibility regardless of AI use.** Every figure in a model is traceable to a sourced
assumption; every recommendation is defensible on its analysis alone.

**(d) Disclosure on request.** We keep a per-engagement AI use log — tool, purpose, what it was
applied to, who reviewed it — available to GCC at any time.

**(e) Compliance with GCC policy.** We will adopt GCC's AI policies and contract terms in full on
award, and brief every person on the engagement, associates included, before they start.

**Confidentiality is the hard line.** Innovator data — financials, cap tables, beneficiary data,
unpublished evidence, anything commercially sensitive — never enters a consumer AI tool or any
service that trains on inputs. We use enterprise configurations with training disabled and retention
controlled, or we do not use AI on that engagement. Where GCC or an innovator prefers no AI
involvement, we deliver without it and record that in the scoping note.

**We never** generate figures presented as findings, fabricate or fill gaps in evidence, let
unreviewed output reach an innovator, or process sensitive data in uncontrolled tools.
