> **Read first.** This is a drafting template built around the way Leap actually works — staged
> studies, financial models, capital raising, interim placements. It is **not legal advice**. Before
> first use, have it reviewed by counsel qualified in the governing jurisdiction, and confirm the
> firm's own details in `01-company-brief.md` §7. Bracketed `[...]` fields are deliberate.

### 1. Definitions

**"Services"** — the services described in Schedule A.
**"Deliverables"** — the reports, models, presentations, data rooms and other outputs listed in
Schedule A.
**"Fees"** — the amounts set out in Schedule B.
**"Client Inputs"** — the information, documents, data, access and approvals listed in Schedule D.
**"Commencement Date"** — the date on which **both** the mobilisation payment under Schedule B and
the Client Inputs marked "before kick-off" in Schedule D have been received.
**"Confidential Information"** — all non-public information of either Party disclosed in connection
with this Agreement, in any form, whether or not marked confidential.
**"Consultant Materials"** — the methodologies, model architectures, templates, algorithms,
simulation frameworks, databases, know-how and software used or developed by the Consultant,
excluding Client Confidential Information.
**"Working Day"** — a day other than Friday, Saturday or a public holiday in `[Egypt]`.

### 2. Scope of Services

2.1 The Consultant shall perform the Services with the reasonable skill, care and diligence
expected of a professional advisory firm, in accordance with Schedule A.
2.2 The Services are **advisory and decision-support** in nature. The Consultant does not perform
statutory audit, formal regulated valuation, legal, tax or engineering services, and nothing in the
Deliverables constitutes such services.
2.3 Anything not expressly stated in Schedule A is outside scope and is dealt with under clause 8
(Change Control).

### 3. Term and Commencement

3.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date above and continues until the Services are completed and
the Fees paid in full, unless terminated earlier under clause 19.
3.2 Performance begins on the Commencement Date. **Durations in Schedule A run from the Commencement
Date, not from signature.**
3.3 Where Client Inputs are delivered late, or approvals are not given within the periods in
Schedule D, the timetable extends day for day and the Consultant is not liable for the resulting
delay. Where the delay exceeds `[20]` Working Days, the Consultant may re-plan the assignment and
charge reasonable, evidenced remobilisation costs.

### 4. Fees, Invoicing and Payment

4.1 The Client shall pay the Fees in Schedule B at the milestones stated there.
4.2 The mobilisation payment is **non-refundable** and is consideration for reserving team capacity
and commencing work.
4.3 Invoices are payable within `[15]` days of the invoice date, in `[currency]`, by transfer to:
`[bank name / account name / account no. / IBAN / SWIFT]`. Bank charges are borne by the Client.
4.4 **Late payment.** Overdue amounts carry interest at `[...]`% per month, or the maximum rate
permitted by law if lower. Where an invoice remains unpaid `[15]` days after its due date, the
Consultant may suspend the Services under clause 19.4 on written notice, without liability.
4.5 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of VAT and any similar tax, which is added where applicable. All
payments are made free of deduction; where the Client is required by law to withhold tax, it shall
gross up the payment so that the Consultant receives the amount it would have received without the
withholding, and shall deliver the withholding certificate within `[30]` days.
4.6 **Expenses.** Travel, accommodation, third-party data subscriptions and field-survey costs are
reimbursed at cost against receipts, subject to prior written approval and the cap in Schedule B.
4.7 **No set-off.** The Client shall pay in full without set-off, counterclaim or deduction.
4.8 Fees are not contingent on the outcome of any decision, transaction, financing or approval,
except where an express success fee is agreed in Schedule B.

### 5. The Consultant's Team

5.1 The engagement principal is `[name]`.
5.2 The Consultant may use employees, subcontractors and associates to perform the Services and
remains responsible for their performance and for their compliance with clause 13.
5.3 The Consultant may substitute team members with personnel of **equivalent seniority and
experience**, notifying the Client in advance where the engagement principal changes.

### 6. Client Obligations

6.1 The Client shall provide the Client Inputs in Schedule D, complete, accurate and on time; give
reasonable access to its personnel, premises and systems; nominate one authorised contact empowered
to approve assumptions and consolidate comments; and respond to requests for approval within `[5]`
Working Days.
6.2 The Client is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of information it supplies. The
Consultant relies on it as provided and does not independently audit or verify it unless expressly
agreed in Schedule A.
6.3 Decisions to invest, divest, price, build, finance or transact remain the Client's alone.

### 7. Deliverables, Acceptance and Revisions

7.1 Deliverables are issued to the recipients named in Schedule A in the formats stated there.
7.2 The Client shall review each Deliverable and give consolidated written comments within `[5]`
Working Days. A Deliverable is **deemed accepted** if no comments are received within that period,
or if the Client uses it for its intended purpose.
7.3 **`[Two]` consolidated revision rounds** per Deliverable are included. Further rounds, or
comments that alter agreed assumptions or scope, are chargeable under clause 8 at the day rates in
Schedule C.
7.4 Where a Deliverable does not conform to Schedule A, the Consultant's obligation is to correct it
within a reasonable period at no further cost. This is the Client's primary remedy for defective
Deliverables.

### 8. Change Control

8.1 Either Party may request a change to the Services. No change is effective until recorded in a
written change order signed by both Parties, stating the change in scope, the effect on the
timetable, and the effect on Fees.
8.2 Work performed at the Client's request outside Schedule A is charged at the day rates in
Schedule C.
8.3 The Consultant is not obliged to start changed work before the change order is signed.

### 9. Nature of the Advice — No Guarantee

9.1 The Deliverables are prepared on the basis of Client Inputs, publicly available information and
market conditions **as at the date of issue**. The Consultant is under no obligation to update them
for later events unless separately engaged.
9.2 Projections, forecasts, feasibility outputs and simulation results are **estimates based on
stated assumptions**. Actual results will differ. The Consultant does not warrant or guarantee any
financial return, internal rate of return, absorption rate, sale price, valuation, financing
outcome, investor interest, transaction completion or regulatory approval.
9.3 Where the Services include investor or lender outreach, the Consultant's obligation is one of
**means, not of result**: to perform the agreed preparation and outreach with professional
diligence. The Consultant does not guarantee that capital will be raised or on what terms.
9.4 The Consultant does not provide legal, tax, audit, accounting, engineering or regulated
valuation advice, and the Client shall obtain such advice from appropriately licensed professionals.

### 10. Reliance and Third Parties

10.1 The Deliverables are prepared **solely for the Client** and solely for the purpose stated in
Schedule A.
10.2 The Client shall not disclose any Deliverable to a third party, nor permit reliance on it,
without the Consultant's prior written consent. Consent may be given subject to the third party
signing a reliance letter on the Consultant's standard terms `[and payment of a reliance fee]`.
10.3 The Consultant accepts no duty of care or liability to any third party who obtains a
Deliverable, whether with or without consent.
10.4 The Client shall not quote from, summarise or extract any Deliverable in a prospectus, offering
memorandum, marketing material or public filing without the Consultant's prior written approval of
the context and wording.

### 11. Intellectual Property

11.1 **Consultant Materials** — including model architectures, simulation frameworks, templates,
methodologies, know-how and databases — remain the exclusive property of the Consultant, together
with all improvements made during the engagement.
11.2 On **payment in full**, the Client receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable, perpetual
licence to use the Deliverables internally for the purpose stated in Schedule A, and to share them
with its shareholders, lenders and professional advisers on a confidential basis, subject to
clause 10.
11.3 Until payment in full, no licence is granted and the Client shall not use the Deliverables.
11.4 The Client retains ownership of its own data and materials and grants the Consultant a licence
to use them for the purposes of performing the Services.
11.5 The Consultant may use the general knowledge, skills and experience gained on the engagement in
other work, provided no Client Confidential Information is disclosed.

### 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each Party shall keep the other's Confidential Information confidential, use it only for the
purposes of this Agreement, and disclose it only to those of its personnel and advisers who need it
and are bound by equivalent obligations.
12.2 The obligation does not apply to information that is or becomes public without breach, was
lawfully known before disclosure, is independently developed, or must be disclosed by law,
regulation or court order — in which case the disclosing Party shall, where lawful, give prior
notice.
12.3 These obligations survive for `[3]` years after termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets
and personal data.
12.4 **Publicity.** Neither Party shall issue a public announcement about the engagement without the
other's written consent. `[The Client grants the Consultant the right to name the Client and
describe the assignment in general terms in its credentials and company profile, subject to the
Client's prior written approval of the wording.]`

### 13. Data Protection

13.1 Each Party shall comply with applicable data protection law, including `[Egypt's Personal Data
Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 and its executive regulations]`.
13.2 Where the Consultant processes personal data on the Client's behalf, it shall do so only on
documented instructions, apply appropriate technical and organisational security measures, impose
equivalent obligations on sub-processors, assist with data-subject requests so far as reasonable,
notify the Client without undue delay of a personal data breach affecting Client data, and delete or
return the data on termination.

### 14. Independence and Conflicts

14.1 The Consultant advises multiple clients, including parties on **both sides** of investment
transactions (company-side preparation and investor-side due diligence).
14.2 The Consultant maintains conflict-check procedures and shall notify the Client promptly if it
identifies a conflict affecting this engagement.
14.3 The Consultant shall not act for both sides of the **same transaction** without prior written
disclosure to, and consent from, both parties, and appropriate information barriers.
14.4 Subject to clause 12, nothing restricts the Consultant from advising other clients, including
competitors of the Client.

### 15. Warranties

15.1 Each Party warrants that it has authority to enter into this Agreement and that doing so does
not breach any other obligation.
15.2 The Consultant warrants that it will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care.
15.3 Save as expressly stated, all warranties, conditions and terms implied by statute or common law
are excluded to the fullest extent permitted by law.

### 16. Liability

16.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, fraudulent misrepresentation, wilful
misconduct, gross negligence, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or any liability that
cannot be limited by law.
16.2 Subject to 16.1, neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit,
loss of revenue, loss of opportunity, loss of anticipated savings, loss of goodwill, or loss arising
from decisions taken by the Client on the basis of the Deliverables.
16.3 Subject to 16.1, the Consultant's total aggregate liability arising out of or in connection
with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, shall not
exceed **`[100]`% of the Fees actually paid** to the Consultant under this Agreement
`[alternative: the lower of the Fees paid and [amount]]`.
16.4 The Consultant is not liable for loss caused by inaccurate, incomplete or late Client Inputs,
by decisions of the Client or third parties, or by changes in market, regulatory or economic
conditions after the date of a Deliverable.
16.5 Any claim must be notified in writing within `[12]` months of the date of the Deliverable or
event giving rise to it, failing which it is barred.

### 17. Non-Solicitation

17.1 During the term and for `[12]` months afterwards, the Client shall not, directly or indirectly,
solicit or employ any individual engaged by the Consultant in the Services without the Consultant's
written consent.
17.2 If the Client engages such an individual in breach of 17.1, it shall pay the Consultant an
introduction fee equal to `[50]`% of that individual's first-year total remuneration.
17.3 This clause applies with particular force to personnel placed under interim management
mandates (service SF-06).

### 18. Anti-Bribery, Sanctions and AML

18.1 Each Party shall comply with all applicable anti-bribery, anti-corruption, anti-money
laundering and sanctions laws, and shall not offer or accept any improper payment in connection with
this Agreement.
18.2 The Client shall provide the customer due diligence information the Consultant reasonably
requires, including beneficial ownership and source of funds.
18.3 The Consultant may suspend or terminate immediately, without liability, if it reasonably
believes continued performance would breach 18.1 or expose it to sanctions risk.

### 19. Termination and Suspension

19.1 Either Party may terminate for convenience on `[30]` days' written notice.
19.2 Either Party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach
that is not remedied within `[15]` Working Days of notice, or becomes insolvent, enters liquidation,
or has a receiver or administrator appointed.
19.3 On termination, the Client shall pay for all Services performed to the termination date,
including work in progress valued at the day rates in Schedule C, plus committed non-cancellable
third-party costs. The mobilisation payment is not refunded.
19.4 The Consultant may suspend the Services on written notice for non-payment (clause 4.4), for
failure to provide Client Inputs (clause 3.3), or under clause 18.3. Suspension does not relieve the
Client of payment obligations already accrued, and the timetable extends by the period of suspension.
19.5 Clauses 4, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 16, 17, 21 and 22 survive termination.

### 20. Force Majeure

Neither Party is liable for failure or delay caused by an event beyond its reasonable control,
including natural disaster, epidemic, war, civil unrest, government action, or failure of essential
utilities or communications networks. The affected Party shall notify the other promptly. Where the
event continues for more than `[60]` days, either Party may terminate on written notice, with
payment due for Services performed to that date.

### 21. Notices

21.1 Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses and email addresses in Schedule E.
21.2 Notice by email is effective on the Working Day of transmission where sent before 17:00 local
time in the recipient's location, and otherwise on the next Working Day, provided no delivery
failure is received.

### 22. General

22.1 **Independent contractor.** The Consultant acts as an independent contractor. Nothing creates a
partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship, and neither Party may bind the other.
22.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent,
not to be unreasonably withheld. The Consultant may subcontract under clause 5.2.
22.3 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement and its Schedules are the entire agreement between the
Parties on its subject matter and supersede all prior proposals, discussions and understandings.
Where the proposal referenced in Schedule A conflicts with these terms, **these terms prevail**.
22.4 **Variation.** Only in writing, signed by both Parties (or by signed change order under
clause 8).
22.5 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remainder continues in force and the
invalid provision is replaced by a valid one of nearest effect.
22.6 **Waiver.** Failure to enforce a right is not a waiver of it.
22.7 **Third-party rights.** No person other than the Parties has any right to enforce this
Agreement.
22.8 **Counterparts and electronic signature.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and by
electronic signature or scanned copy, each of which is an original.
22.9 **Language.** This Agreement is executed in Arabic and English. `[The Arabic text prevails in
the event of any inconsistency.]`

### 23. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

23.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of `[the Arab Republic of Egypt]`.
23.2 The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith negotiation between senior
representatives within `[30]` days of written notice of the dispute.
23.3 Failing resolution, the dispute shall be **finally settled by arbitration** under the rules of
`[the Cairo Regional Centre for International Commercial Arbitration (CRCICA)]` by `[one]`
arbitrator, seated in `[Cairo]`, conducted in `[Arabic]`. The award is final and binding.
> **Alternatives to select at drafting:** for Saudi counterparties, `[the Saudi Center for
> Commercial Arbitration (SCCA), seated in Riyadh]`; for UAE/international counterparties,
> `[DIFC-LCIA / arbitration under DIAC rules, seated in Dubai]`; or, for small domestic engagements,
> `[the exclusive jurisdiction of the economic courts of Cairo]`.
