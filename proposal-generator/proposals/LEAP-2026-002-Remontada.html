3. Understanding of Client Needs

Client Background

Remontada is an early-stage technology startup led by Abdelrhman Alqarney. The business is at the foundational stage of its journey — with a core concept and initial momentum, but without the formal strategic, governance, and financial infrastructure required to scale sustainably, manage a growing team, and engage investors or partners with confidence.

Problem Statement

Operating without a defined corporate strategy exposes Remontada to reactive decision-making and misaligned priorities. The absence of a governance framework creates ambiguity in roles, accountability, and decision rights — a risk that intensifies as the team and stakeholder base grows. Without a formal business plan and financial model, the company lacks the roadmap and metrics needed to manage execution and communicate value to investors. These gaps are not uncommon at this stage, but they must be addressed before the business scales further.

Engagement Objectives

  • Define Remontada's corporate strategy including vision, mission, competitive positioning, and strategic priorities
  • Design and document a governance framework covering organizational structure, decision-making authority, and operational protocols
  • Develop a comprehensive business plan translating strategy into a structured, actionable roadmap
  • Build a financial plan and model covering revenue projections, cost structure, cash flow, and funding requirements
  • Provide ongoing monthly advisory support during the three months following delivery to ensure effective implementation

4. Proposed Approach & Methodology

Our approach is structured across three sequential phases, moving from understanding to strategy to documentation and financial validation. Every deliverable is built collaboratively with Remontada's leadership — we do not hand over reports; we build tools together.

PhaseNameDescriptionDuration
1Discovery & DiagnosticDeep-dive into the business model, market, team, and current state to establish a clear baseline2 weeks
2Strategy & Governance DevelopmentDefine corporate strategy and design the governance framework3 weeks
3Business Plan & Financial ModelDevelop the business plan and build the financial model3 weeks

Phase 1 — Discovery & Diagnostic

  • Structured leadership interviews to understand vision, market assumptions, and current operations
  • Business model canvas mapping and competitive landscape review
  • Gap assessment: what exists, what is missing, what is misaligned

Phase 2 — Strategy & Governance Development

  • Strategy workshop with founding team to align on vision, positioning, and strategic priorities
  • Corporate strategy document: mission, vision, goals, competitive moat, and growth levers
  • Governance framework: org structure, role definitions, decision-making matrix, board/advisory protocols

Phase 3 — Business Plan & Financial Model

  • Business plan: executive summary, market analysis, product/service model, go-to-market strategy, operational plan, and management section
  • Financial model: 3-year P&L, cash flow statement, revenue build-up, cost structure, break-even analysis, and funding requirements
  • Final review session and handover
