## 3. Understanding of Client Needs

**Client Background**

AlNour Food Industries Co. is an Egyptian food manufacturer with over 15 years of operations, producing a range of packaged food products distributed across Egypt's modern and traditional trade channels. The company has built strong brand equity domestically and maintains ISO-certified production facilities capable of meeting export quality standards.

**Problem Statement**

Despite strong domestic performance, AlNour has yet to systematically pursue export markets, resulting in missed revenue diversification and exposure to Egypt's macroeconomic volatility. Informal export attempts to Saudi Arabia and the UAE have not been supported by structured market entry planning, leaving the company without a clear understanding of optimal market positioning, pricing architecture, distributor selection criteria, or regulatory compliance requirements in target markets.

**Engagement Objectives**

- Assess the commercial attractiveness and competitive dynamics of the KSA and UAE markets for AlNour's product portfolio
- Define a prioritized market entry strategy with clear positioning, channel, and partnership recommendations
- Build a five-year financial feasibility model covering revenue projections, cost structure, and return on investment under base, optimistic, and downside scenarios
- Identify and assess potential distribution partners in each target market
- Deliver a board-ready presentation summarizing findings and recommendations

## 4. Proposed Approach & Methodology

Our methodology is structured around three sequential phases, each building on the last to move from diagnosis to strategy to financial validation. We do not produce generic reports — every output is tailored to AlNour's specific products, competitive position, and organizational capacity.

| Phase | Name | Description | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery & Market Diagnostics | Baseline AlNour's current export readiness; conduct desk research and expert interviews on KSA and UAE FMCG markets | 3 weeks |
| 2 | Strategy Development | Define market entry strategy, channel architecture, pricing model, and distributor shortlist for each market | 4 weeks |
| 3 | Financial Modeling & Finalization | Build full financial feasibility model; integrate strategy findings; prepare board presentation and final report | 3 weeks |

**Phase 1 — Discovery & Market Diagnostics**
- Internal diagnostic: product portfolio, pricing, production capacity, existing export experience
- Desk research on KSA and UAE food import regulations, labeling requirements, and tariff structures
- Competitive mapping: key regional and international players in AlNour's categories

**Phase 2 — Strategy Development**
- Market prioritization framework: scoring KSA vs. UAE across 8 strategic dimensions
- Channel strategy: modern trade vs. specialty retail vs. e-commerce vs. foodservice
- Distributor identification: long-list of 10–15 potential partners per market, evaluated against defined criteria
- Pricing architecture: recommended retail and wholesale pricing with margin analysis

**Phase 3 — Financial Modeling & Finalization**
- Five-year P&L model with revenue build-up by market, channel, and SKU cluster
- Investment requirements: working capital, regulatory costs, marketing budget
- Scenario analysis: base, upside, downside with sensitivity tables
- Board presentation: executive narrative + visual summary deck
