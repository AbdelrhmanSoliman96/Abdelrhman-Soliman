Everything stated is something StartPad demonstrably does — which matters more on LinkedIn than anywhere else, because this is the page investors, mentors and partners check before replying to you.

## What I deliberately left out

Worth knowing, because each of these would be tempting:

- **"Join thousands of entrepreneurs."** You have ~104 founders. On LinkedIn — where mentors, partners and investors read your page — an unverifiable number is worse than a small true one. If you want a figure, use the real one.
- **"The #1 gamified platform."** An unsupportable superiority claim, and the same one flagged on the contact page.
- **Investor introductions.** Not something StartPad currently controls, and the single most consequential thing to overpromise to founders.
- **A mentor count.** Two mentors is thin. Say mentors exist and are bookable — don't quantify until it flatters.
- **Founding-team credentials.** If any founder has a notable background, one line about it belongs here and would be the strongest addition to this page. I don't have that detail.

## Two additions worth making

**Add the team.** LinkedIn weights company pages by associated employees. Everyone on the team should list StartPad as their employer — it materially affects how the page surfaces in search and in the feed.

**Keep the tagline and About in sync with the site.** The pricing sweep in Round 12 changes what "free" means. When missions 1–3 becomes the free tier everywhere, this page already says so — but if the tier changes again, this is one more place to update.
