Overview / About — 2,000 character limit

Paste as one message
Most good ideas in this region die in a group chat.

Not because they're bad — because nobody teaches the actual steps. University teaches theory. YouTube teaches hype. Neither shows you how to validate an idea, run a real customer interview, build an MVP, or get ready for an investor conversation. Not in Arabic. Not for our markets.

StartPad is the guided path we wished existed.

HOW IT WORKS

15 missions take you from a raw idea to a launched startup, across eight phases: Discovery, Analysis, Ideation, Validation, Business Model, Development, Strategy and Launch.

Each mission asks for real work — interviews, evidence, decisions. Every submission is assessed against five criteria: specificity, depth, evidence, actionability and relevance. You need 70% to pass. The feedback quotes your own answers back to you and names exactly what to fix. Resubmit as many times as it takes.

WHAT COMES WITH IT

· A toolkit of 114 calculators, models and canvases — P&L, burn and runway, cap table, unit economics, go-to-market
· AI tools that draft interview questions and surveys for your specific market
· Mentors you book by the session, in Arabic or English
· A community scoped to the mission you're on, so you're always talking to founders at your stage
· A knowledge library written for MENA — not translated from somewhere else

WHO IT'S FOR

First-time founders in Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and across the Arab world. Students, people with full-time jobs, people building on the side. No experience required. You don't even need an idea to start.

Missions 1–3 are free, no card required.

Built in Cairo. Designed for the entire Arab world.

What I deliberately left out

Worth knowing, because each of these would be tempting:

- "Join thousands of entrepreneurs." You have ~104 founders. On LinkedIn — where mentors, partners and investors read your page — an unverifiable number is worse than a small true one. If you want a figure, use the real one. - "The #1 gamified platform." An unsupportable superiority claim, and the same one flagged on the contact page. - Investor introductions. Not something StartPad currently controls, and the single most consequential thing to overpromise to founders. - A mentor count. Two mentors is thin. Say mentors exist and are bookable — don't quantify until it flatters. - Founding-team credentials. If any founder has a notable background, one line about it belongs here and would be the strongest addition to this page. I don't have that detail.

Two additions worth making

Add the team. LinkedIn weights company pages by associated employees. Everyone on the team should list StartPad as their employer — it materially affects how the page surfaces in search and in the feed.

Keep the tagline and About in sync with the site. The pricing sweep in Round 12 changes what "free" means. When missions 1–3 becomes the free tier everywhere, this page already says so — but if the tier changes again, this is one more place to update.
