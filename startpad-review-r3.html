Three legal exposures

1 · Collecting personal data with no privacy policy

Sign-up takes first name, last name, phone number, email and password. There is no privacy policy, no terms, and no consent checkbox. The footer offers About and Contact only.

Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (151/2020), Saudi PDPL and the UAE federal law all require a privacy notice and a stated lawful basis before collection. Google and Apple both require a published privacy policy in their sign-in developer terms — your OAuth integrations are out of compliance today. App stores reject without one.

You're also now storing AI conversation transcripts, uploaded files, screenshots and PDFs inside missions. That materially raises what the notice has to cover.

2 · The Slack / Google / Amazon / HubSpot logo wall

Under "BUILT ON THE GROWTH & STARTUP FRAMEWORKS BEHIND", next to Reforge. A logo wall reads as customers, partners or investors — that is what the pattern means, and the qualifying phrase doesn't undo it. Using those marks in a way that implies affiliation is actionable, and all four enforce. It also fails with the audience you want: anyone who recognises Reforge knows you aren't affiliated with it.

Remove it. Name the methodologies in text instead — jobs-to-be-done, lean validation, growth loops. True, defensible, free.

3 · The landing page contradicts its own numbers

"Founders 104+" in the hero. "Join thousands of entrepreneurs" in the closing CTA. Same page. Plus "founders from 50+ countries" against 104 founders, and "2,500+ successful startups" unsourced.

104 is a good number for an early product and it's the one you can prove. Every unsupportable claim beside it makes the provable one look invented.

Your ask · profile fields

Age and current status

Both are straightforward, but each carries a consequence cheaper to handle now than to retrofit.

Store date of birth, not age

An age integer is wrong within a year and can't self-correct. Store DOB, derive age at read time.

The consequence: once you know age, you know when a user is a minor — and a Gen Z founder platform will have 16- and 17-year-olds. Under Egypt's PDPL, Saudi PDPL and GDPR-equivalent regimes, processing a minor's data needs guardian consent, and public posting, mentor video calls and investor introductions carry real duty-of-care weight. Set a minimum age deliberately and enforce it at sign-up.

Make status a product input, not a demographic field

Options, ordered for this audience: Student · Recent graduate · Employed full-time · Employed part-time · Freelancer / self-employed · Founder (full-time) · Founder (alongside another commitment) · Between roles · Other.

Why it earns its place: paired with one more question — hours per week available — it's the strongest predictor you'll have of who finishes the journey, and it lets you pace missions realistically instead of giving a full-time founder and a student in exam season the same weekly goal. Your weekly goal is currently a flat 3 missions for everyone.

Set 3 covers both, including minor handling and where the fields sit in the existing 63% completion banner — which should absorb them rather than growing a second nag.
