## 5. Investor Introductions, Client Engagement and Attribution

**5.1 Introduced Investor.** An Investor is an "Introduced Investor" if the Consultant was the effective cause of the Client's contact with that Investor, including where the Consultant:

- **(a)** made or arranged an introduction, referral or first contact, directly or through a third party;
- **(b)** included the Investor on the Target Investor Map;
- **(c)** included the Investor in an outreach wave, communication or campaign conducted under Phase 2;
- **(d)** provided the Client with the Investor's identity or contact details in circumstances where the Client was not already in active discussion with that Investor; or
- **(e)** materially facilitated, brokered or re-activated the relationship where contact had previously lapsed,

in each case where the relevant event is recorded in the Investor Log in accordance with Clause 5.2.

**5.2 Investor Log.** The Consultant shall maintain the Investor Log in the form at Schedule 3, recording for each Investor the name, source, date of first contact and attribution. The Investor Log shall be shared with the Client **weekly** with the pipeline report. The Client may dispute any attribution entry by written notice within **seven (7) Business Days** of receipt of the report in which it first appears; absent such notice, the entry is final and binding. This mechanism exists so that attribution is settled contemporaneously and never retrospectively.

**5.3 Engagement Standard.** In respect of every Introduced Investor, the Client shall:

- **(a)** respond substantively to any communication within **three (3) Business Days**;
- **(b)** attend scheduled meetings, subject to no more than one (1) rescheduling per Investor;
- **(c)** provide requested diligence materials within **five (5) Business Days**;
- **(d)** notify the Consultant within **two (2) Business Days** of any direct contact with an Introduced Investor, and copy or brief the Consultant on material correspondence; and
- **(e)** deal with each Introduced Investor professionally and in good faith.

**5.4 Non-Engagement.** Two (2) or more failures to meet the Engagement Standard, or any single failure continuing for more than ten (10) Business Days, constitutes "**Non-Engagement**". Non-Engagement is treated by the Parties as evidence that the Client is not seriously pursuing the Round. On Non-Engagement, the Consultant may, by written notice:

- **(a)** suspend outreach and all Phase 2 activity, so as not to expose the Consultant's investor relationships to an unresponsive counterparty;
- **(b)** stop the timeline clock under Clause 7.2, with all affected dates extending day-for-day;
- **(c)** require the Client to cure within ten (10) Business Days; and
- **(d)** if the Client does not cure, terminate under Clause 8.3, in which case all Fees accrued to the date of termination fall due immediately, and Clauses 6.2 and 9 continue to apply throughout the Tail Period.

**5.5 Non-circumvention.** The Client shall not, and shall procure that its founders, shareholders, officers, employees, affiliates and any entity under common control shall not, directly or indirectly, negotiate with, transact with, or accept investment from an Introduced Investor otherwise than under this Agreement, or in any manner structured to avoid or reduce the Success Fee. Any investment received in breach of this Clause is deemed to attract the Success Fee in full.

**5.6 Attribution survives.** The Success Fee is payable on any Ticket funded by an Introduced Investor whether the Ticket is funded during the Term or at any time within the Tail Period, and whether the final negotiation was conducted by the Consultant or by the Client.

**5.7 No warranty as to Investors.** The Consultant makes no representation or warranty as to the financial standing, willingness, capacity or conduct of any Investor. An introduction is not a recommendation to accept investment, and the Client is responsible for conducting its own diligence on any Investor before accepting funds.

**5.8 Client-sourced investors.** For the avoidance of doubt, an Investor sourced independently by the Client, not appearing on the Target Investor Map, not included in any outreach wave and not otherwise falling within Clause 5.1, is a "Client-Sourced Investor". **No Success Fee is payable on a Client-Sourced Investor's Ticket.** The M4 Closing Milestone under Clause 6.1 remains payable regardless of whether the First Close arises from an Introduced Investor or a Client-Sourced Investor.

## 6. Fees and Payment

**6.1 Fixed Fee.** The Client shall pay the Consultant a Fixed Fee of **EGP 150,000 (One Hundred and Fifty Thousand Egyptian Pounds)**, as follows:

| Milestone | Trigger | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| **M1 — Down payment** | On signature of this Agreement | **EGP 30,000** |
| **M2 — Readiness** | On completion of Phase 1 under Clause 3.4 | **EGP 30,000** |
| **M3 — Round** | On launch of the first outreach wave under Phase 2 | **EGP 20,000** |
| **M4 — Closing** | On First Close | **EGP 70,000** |

**(a)** The M1 down payment is a non-refundable mobilisation fee and is payable before the Phase 1 Start Date.

**(b)** The M3 Round Milestone Fee of EGP 20,000 shall become due on the date the Consultant launches the first outreach wave under Phase 2 Stage 2, and is payable whether or not any Investor has by then responded, met the Client or begun diligence.

**(c)** The M4 Closing Milestone Fee of EGP 70,000 shall become due upon First Close — being the date on which the first Investment Amount is received in cleared funds into the Client's bank account — irrespective of whether the Round remains open for subsequent tranches, and **irrespective of whether the closing Investor was an Introduced Investor or a Client-Sourced Investor**.

**(d)** If the Round has not reached First Close by the date this Agreement expires or is terminated other than under Clause 8.2, M4 remains payable on the first subsequent close occurring within the Tail Period.

**6.2 Success Fee.** In addition to the Fixed Fee, the Client shall pay the Consultant a Success Fee of **ten percent (10%) of the aggregate Investment Amount funded by Introduced Investors**.

- **(a)** The Success Fee accrues per Ticket and is calculated on amounts actually received by the Client in cleared funds. Where a Ticket funds in tranches, the Success Fee is payable pro rata as each tranche is received.
- **(b)** No Success Fee is payable in respect of a Client-Sourced Investor.
- **(c)** Where consideration is received other than in cash, the Success Fee is calculated on the fair market value of that consideration as at the date of receipt, agreed between the Parties or, failing agreement within ten (10) Business Days, determined by an independent valuer jointly appointed and shared equally in cost.
- **(d)** Where an Investment Amount is denominated in a currency other than Egyptian Pounds, it shall be converted at the Central Bank of Egypt mid-market rate on the date the funds are received in cleared funds.
- **(e)** The Success Fee applies to Tickets funded during the Term and throughout the Tail Period, in accordance with Clauses 5.6 and 9.

**6.3 Invoicing and payment.** The Consultant shall issue an invoice on each Fee becoming due. Each invoice is payable within **seven (7) Business Days** of receipt, by bank transfer to the account nominated by the Consultant in writing. Payment obligations are not conditional on the Client's receipt of any third-party funds other than as expressly stated in the relevant trigger.

**6.4 Late payment.** Sums unpaid after the due date carry interest at **one and a half percent (1.5%) per month** on the outstanding balance, accruing daily from the due date until payment. Where any invoice remains unpaid for more than fifteen (15) Business Days, the Consultant may suspend the Services on written notice, without prejudice to any other right.

**6.5 No set-off.** All Fees are payable in full without set-off, counterclaim, deduction or withholding, save as required by law.

**6.6 Taxes.** All Fees are stated exclusive of Value Added Tax and any other applicable indirect tax, which the Client shall pay in addition where legally chargeable. Where the Client is required by Egyptian law to deduct withholding tax from a payment, it may do so, shall account for it to the Egyptian Tax Authority, and shall deliver to the Consultant the official withholding certificate within thirty (30) days of deduction. Each Party is responsible for its own income taxes.

**6.7 Expenses.** Fees exclude third-party costs — data room software subscriptions, paid market data, design licences, printing, and travel outside Greater Cairo. Such costs are chargeable at cost, and only where approved by the Client in writing in advance.

**6.8 Currency.** All Fees are payable in Egyptian Pounds unless otherwise agreed in writing.
