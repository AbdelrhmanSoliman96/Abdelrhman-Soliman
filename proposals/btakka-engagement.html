PART I — PROPOSAL

1. Executive Summary

Btakka operates two published iOS products on the Egyptian App Store — Btakka Business (App Store) and Btakka Finishing & Decor (App Store). A live product in market is the hardest part of a pre-seed story, and Btakka already has it.

What most pre-seed companies in this position lack is not a product and not ambition — it is the evidence package. Investors do not fund a good idea described well. They fund a company that can answer every question in a diligence checklist within twenty-four hours, with numbers that reconcile, a story that holds under pressure, and a founder who has clearly done this work before the meeting rather than during it.

This engagement delivers exactly that, in two phases:

Phase 1 — Investment Readiness (1 month). Build the complete pre-seed data room against the 36-item checklist, the financial model and financial expectations, the valuation, the pitch deck, the teaser, the business plan and the full investor document set. Delivered through four structured workshops that transfer the reasoning to the founding team, not just the files.

Phase 2 — Investment Round Management (3 months). Convert readiness into capital: fundraising strategy, investor target map, structured outreach, meeting management, diligence coordination, term sheet negotiation and close.

Commercials. EGP 150,000 in fixed fees across three milestones (EGP 30,000 on signature, EGP 30,000 on completion of readiness, EGP 90,000 on first close), plus a 10% Success Fee on the value of investment tickets funded by investors the Consultant introduced.

The one thing that determines whether this works. Every deadline in this document is conditional on Btakka's engagement — documents delivered on time, workshops attended by decision-makers, and introduced investors answered within three business days. This is stated plainly in Clause 5 and Clause 7 because it is the single largest cause of failed rounds, and it is the one input the Consultant cannot supply.

4. Engagement Objective

To take Btakka from its current position to a funded pre-seed round, by:

  1. Producing an investor-grade data room covering all 36 items of the Pre-Seed Round Data Room Checklist.
  2. Producing a defensible financial model, financial expectations and valuation that withstand investor scrutiny.
  3. Producing the complete investor document set — pitch deck, teaser, business plan, term sheet template and valuation.
  4. Equipping the founding team, through workshops, to own and defend every number and every claim.
  5. Running a structured, time-boxed fundraising process from strategy through outreach to signed close.

What "success" means in this document. Phase 1 succeeds when the data room passes a diligence readiness review and is signed off (Schedule A). Phase 2 succeeds when investment funds are received in cleared funds in Btakka's account. The Consultant does not, and cannot, guarantee that a round will be funded — see Clause 12.

5. Scope of Work

Phase 1 — Investment Readiness (Weeks 1–4)

Workstream A — Story, Positioning & Market

  • Investment thesis and narrative spine for Btakka
  • Problem/solution framing calibrated to pre-seed investor expectations
  • Market sizing: TAM, SAM and SOM with sourced, defensible methodology
  • Competitive analysis and differentiation map
  • Go-to-market strategy and customer acquisition plan
  • Customer development evidence pack and traction narrative

Workstream B — Financial Model, Expectations & Valuation

  • Full driver-based financial model, 3–5 year horizon, monthly for year 1–2
  • Revenue model across both product lines, built on real unit economics
  • Cost structure: COGS, operating expenses, and headcount costs derived from Btakka's HR plan
  • Cash flow, burn rate, runway and current cash position statement
  • Base / conservative / upside scenarios with sensitivity analysis
  • Financial expectations pack — the forward projections investors will underwrite
  • Use of Funds statement covering a 12–18 month runway with milestone mapping
  • Pre-seed valuation with method, comparables and a defensible range
  • Current and post-money cap table, including dilution scenarios
  • Key metrics dashboard and operational KPI set

Workstream C — Data Room Construction

  • Full data room architecture, folder structure, naming convention and index
  • Preparation, drafting or investor-formatting of every Consultant-owned item in Schedule A
  • Collection, review and quality control of every Client-provided item in Schedule A
  • Gap analysis and a remediation list for anything missing, weak or inconsistent
  • Diligence Q&A bank — the questions investors will ask, with prepared answers
  • Access control, permissioning and version discipline
  • Final diligence readiness review and sign-off

Workstream D — Investor Document Set

  • Pitch deck — 10–15 slides: problem, solution, market, product, traction, business model, competition, team, financials, the ask
  • Teaser — one-page designed summary for first-touch outreach
  • Business plan — full narrative document, all parts
  • Term sheet template — proposed investment terms reflecting Btakka's valuation expectations
  • Valuation document — method, assumptions, comparables and conclusion
  • Founder bios written to investor standard, from the CVs provided
  • Product demo script and walkthrough structure built around the app screenshots provided

Phase 2 — Investment Round Management (Months 2–4)

Stage 1 — Fundraising Strategy (Weeks 5–6)

  • Round architecture: size, instrument (equity / SAFE / convertible note), valuation and terms
  • Investor segmentation: angels, syndicates, pre-seed funds, family offices, strategic and corporate investors, accelerators and relevant Egyptian and regional programmes
  • Target Investor Map with prioritised tiers, thesis fit and warm-path mapping
  • Outreach infrastructure: sequencing, messaging, channel plan and tracking
  • Pitch rehearsal and objection handling with the founding team
  • Round timeline with weekly milestones and a defined close target

Stage 2 — Outreach & Investor Engagement (Weeks 7–12)

  • Structured outreach waves against the Target Investor Map
  • Warm introductions through the Consultant's network where available
  • Teaser and deck distribution under version and access control
  • Meeting scheduling, preparation briefs and post-meeting debriefs
  • Investor feedback captured, analysed and fed back into the materials
  • Weekly pipeline reporting: contacted, engaged, in diligence, term sheet, closed
  • Iteration of deck, model and data room in response to real investor objections

Stage 3 — Diligence, Negotiation & Close (Weeks 13–16)

  • Diligence management: request coordination, response drafting, data room administration
  • Investor question handling and escalation
  • Term sheet review, commercial commentary and negotiation support
  • Valuation and dilution modelling across competing offers
  • Syndicate construction where multiple investors participate
  • Coordination with Btakka's legal counsel through to signing and funding
  • Post-close investor reporting structure and investor update template

12. Why This Engagement

The work is done before the meeting, not during it. Most pre-seed companies start assembling a data room after an investor asks for one. By then the momentum is gone. This engagement front-loads the entire evidence package into four weeks so that outreach begins from a position of complete readiness.

The numbers are built to be attacked. Fifty-plus models built and reviewed means the model Btakka receives has already been stress-tested against the specific objections pre-seed investors raise in this market.

The founders keep the capability. The workshop structure exists so that Btakka's team can defend every assumption after the engagement ends — in this round and in the next one.

The incentives are aligned. EGP 90,000 of the fixed fee and the entire Success Fee sit behind a close. The Consultant is paid properly when Btakka is funded.

The process is transparent. Weekly pipeline reporting and a live Investor Log mean Btakka always knows exactly where the round stands.
