Fix these before anything else

Six things that are broken, not debatable

Everything else in this document is a design judgement you can push back on. These six are defects visible to any user who lands on the page today.

1 · Mentoring shows past sessions as "upcoming"

Today is 11 August 2026. The page lists sessions on 27 May 2026 and 9 June 2026 under "Your Upcoming Sessions", both badged upcoming — two and a half months in the past. Meanwhile the only genuinely future session (13 Aug) is badged pending. The status is derived wrong or never recomputed. This is the most damaging bug in the set: a founder who sees a stale calendar stops trusting the booking system entirely.

2 · A blank button on the landing page

In the final CTA band ("Your startup starts here"), next to "Go to dashboard" there is a white button with no label at all. Missing translation string or a failed conditional render. It's the last thing a visitor sees before the footer.

3 · Test data is live in the Collaboration Hub

Projects named projx and dasd, a description reading dsad, and a card that says No description. This is the first thing a new user sees on that page, and it reads as an abandoned product. Purge it, or gate the page behind an empty state until there is real content.

4 · "1 Total Posts"

Pluralisation bug on the Community stat cards. Also "0 Questions", "0 Answered" — see finding C-01 for why those cards should not exist yet at all.

5 · Delete is the loudest button on the Collaboration Hub

A full-width, fully-saturated red Delete button is the single most visually dominant element on the screen — larger and louder than "Join", "Team", or anything else. Destructive actions must never out-rank primary ones. One mis-tap on mobile destroys a project.

6 · Logged-in users are served the marketing homepage

A signed-in founder, 63% through their profile, lands on the full public pitch — hero, mission & vision, "Why we built this", MENA market stats, the lot. It's roughly eight screens of scroll to sell a product they have already bought. Redirect authenticated users to /dashboard, or serve a logged-in home.

SCREEN 01

Landing page

Verdict · Strong copy buried under corporate scaffolding

Confirms Round 1's UX-01 and UX-02 outright: there is no product visible anywhere on this page, and no way to see the 15 missions before signing up.

L-01 P0

Your best line is four screens down

"We grew up watching brilliant ideas die in group chats." That is the most persuasive sentence on the entire site — specific, emotional, true to the audience, and unmistakably yours. It sits in "Why we built this", roughly 60% of the way down the page, where almost nobody reaches. Fix: that line, or something built from it, belongs at or near the top. The generic "StartPad turns ideas into startups" headline could describe any of forty products.

L-02 P0

Nothing on the page shows the product

Four feature cards, a mission statement, a vision statement, a founding story, three "built different" cards, and market stats — and not one screenshot, mission map, or interactive moment. A visitor cannot form a mental picture of what using StartPad is like. Fix: replace the "Our mission / Our vision" block with the actual 15-mission path, and put a live interactive element in the hero.

L-03 P0

"15+" contradicts your own copy, twice

The stat band says 15+. The feature card below says "15 step-by-step missions". The story section says "Fifteen real missions". Fix: it is 15. The "+" reads as padding and costs you the credibility that a precise number buys. Say 15 everywhere.

L-04 P1

"MENA" is not a statistic

The stat row runs 15+ / 24/7 / 100% / MENA. Three numbers and a word, in a component whose whole grammar is numbers. Fix: make it a real figure — countries served, founders enrolled, missions completed this month.

L-05 P1

Mission & vision statements are About-page content

"To democratise startup building…" / "To make StartPad the #1 startup platform in the Arab world" — this is language written for investors and grant committees, not for a 21-year-old deciding whether to click. It also sits above the founding story, which is far more compelling. Fix: move both to /about.

L-06 P1

"100% FREE TO START" with no pricing page

"To start" implies something later costs money, and there is no pricing link anywhere on the page or in the footer. For the most fraud-literate cohort online, an implied paid tier with hidden pricing reads as a trap. Fix: either say "free, permanently" and mean it, or ship /pricing and link it in the nav and footer.

L-07 P1

Market stats carry no sources

400M+ people, 70% under 35, $3.5B VC funding (2024) — strong, relevant figures presented with no attribution. Fix: a small source line under each. It costs one line and converts a claim into evidence.

L-08 P1

Social links skip the channels this audience lives on

Footer offers LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook. For MENA Gen Z the order should be Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp channel, X — with LinkedIn kept for mentors and partners. Facebook is close to irrelevant for under-25s in this region.

L-09 P2

Hero subhead is three lines of throat-clearing

"StartPad is a gamified, AI-powered platform that guides young entrepreneurs in the MENA region through every step of building a real startup — from first idea to first customer." Fix: one line. "From your first idea to your first customer, in 15 missions." Let the eyebrow carry the MENA framing, which it already does well.

L-10 P2

What works — keep it

The "MADE IN EGYPT · BUILT FOR MENA" eyebrow, the two-tone headline treatment, "Built in Cairo. Designed for the entire Arab world.", the type pairing, and the restraint of the green palette. The visual identity is coherent and it does not look templated. The problem here is structure and priority, not craft.

SCREEN 02

Collaboration Hub

Verdict · The weakest screen — reads as abandoned
H-01 bug P0

Junk data is the entire content of the page

projx, dasd, dsad, "No description." Two cards, both garbage. Fix: delete the test rows and design a real empty state — "No projects yet. Start one and find people to build it with." plus the New Project button. An honest empty state is far better than fake activity.

H-02 bug P0

Delete outranks every other action on screen

Full-width, fully saturated red, bottom of the card, larger than Join. Fix: move it into an overflow menu (⋯) in the card header, styled as tertiary text, with a confirm dialog that names the consequence: "Delete projx? This removes its files and chat for all 2 members. This cannot be undone."

H-03 P0

Two cards for the same object type have completely different anatomy

Card one: a single giant Join button. Card two: five small buttons in a grid plus Delete. Different heights, different rhythm, no shared structure. Fix: one card component. Constant elements (name, description, members, date, status) always in the same place; the action area swaps based on the viewer's role. Add a "Your project" badge so the difference is explained rather than just felt.

H-04 P1

A collaboration hub with no way to say what you need

The page's stated job is "find collaborators" — but no card says what role it is looking for. Fix: "Looking for" chips on every project: designer, developer, marketer, co-founder. Then filters on those chips, plus filters for country and startup stage. That is the entire value of the page and it is currently missing.

H-05 P1

An unlabelled date

"Aug 5, 2026" floats at the card's right edge with no label. Created? Deadline? Last active? Fix: label it, and prefer relative time for recency — "Active 3 days ago" tells a joiner what they need to know; a creation date does not.

H-06 P1

"No description." is a dead end

Fix: for the owner, make it an inline prompt — "Add a description so people know what you're building →". For everyone else, hide the line entirely rather than advertising the gap.

H-07 P1

Five feature buttons where a project page belongs

Team, Files, Whiteboard, Video calls, Activity crammed into a card. Fix: the card gets one action — "Open project" — and those five become tabs inside the project. If any of them are not yet built, remove the button; a dead button costs more trust than a missing feature.

H-08 P1

"My Sessions" here collides with Mentoring

Sessions exist in the Collaboration Hub and on the Mentoring page. Team Chat exists here and Community exists separately. Fix: one home for scheduled things, one home for conversation. Right now a founder has to guess which of three places holds what they are looking for.

SCREEN 03

Community

Verdict · Good bones, actively advertising its own emptiness
C-01 P0

Four large cards whose only job is to say nobody is here

1 Total Posts · 1 Discussions · 0 Questions · 0 Answered. Then "Top Contributors: 1 post". A newcomer's first impression is a ghost town, stated numerically, four times. Fix: remove the stat row until there is real volume. Replace with something that creates presence — "12 founders online now", or the mission each poster is currently on.

C-02 P0

The feed has no connection to missions

This is the structural miss. A founder on Mission 3 opens Community and sees a space-food pitch from 19 days ago. Fix: default the feed to founders on the same mission as you, with tabs for My mission / My circle / All. The mission context is the only thing that makes an early, thin community feel alive — it converts "1 post" into "3 people are on Mission 3 right now".

C-03 P0

Arabic posts render inside a left-to-right shell

The post body is correctly right-aligned, but its container is not: the category chip, author row, metadata, hashtags and action icons all stay LTR, so the card reads as two competing layouts. The hashtag chip renders with the # on the wrong side and a stranded full stop. Fix: detect direction per post and set dir on the whole card, not just the text node. Given the audience, Arabic posts will be the majority — this needs to be right.

C-04 bug P1

"1 Total Posts"

Pluralisation. Fix: use Intl.PluralRules — and note that Arabic has six plural forms, so hard-coded s suffixing will break the moment you localise.

C-05 P1

Public view counts on a quiet feed suppress posting

"4 views · 1 like · 0 comments" tells every prospective poster that writing here is shouting into a void. Fix: hide counts below a threshold. Show them once they flatter rather than deter.

C-06 P1

Categories aren't steering anyone

The one post is an idea pitch filed under Help & Support. Fix: pick the category from the composer's intent rather than a free dropdown — "Share an idea" files to Ideas automatically. Structured composers beat taxonomies that users must interpret.

C-07 P1

Four separate routes to create a post

"+ New Post" centred under the header, plus Quick Actions offering Ask a Question, Share an Idea, Showcase Project. Fix: keep the three intent-led actions — they are the better pattern — and drop the generic New Post button, or make it the container that opens those three.

C-08 P2

The layout doesn't collapse around thin content

One post leaves roughly two thirds of the column empty while the sidebar runs on past it. Fix: when the feed is under three posts, drop the sidebar into the flow below and let the feed take full width.

C-09 P2

What works — keep it

The "Translate to English" toggle with the AR language badge is genuinely excellent and rare. It directly serves a bilingual community and removes the main reason people avoid posting in Arabic. Make it more prominent, and add the reverse direction for English posts.

SCREEN 04

Mentoring

Verdict · Contains the most damaging bug in the product
M-01 bug P0

Sessions from May and June are listed as "upcoming" in August

27 May and 9 June, both badged upcoming, on 11 August. The genuinely future session — 13 August — is badged pending. Fix: derive status from the timestamp at render, not from a stored field. Split the page into Upcoming and Past, sort ascending within each, and add a real state machine: requested → confirmed → completed → cancelled / no-show. A calendar that shows the past as the future is worse than no calendar.

M-02 P0

The page called "Mentoring" offers no way to find a mentor

It shows your bookings and stops. There is no directory, no browse, no search, no "Book a session" button anywhere on screen. Fix: the primary action on this page is Find a mentor. Bookings are secondary. Right now the page can only be used by someone who already booked — which is nobody, on their first visit.

M-03 P0

Every mentor name is Western, on a platform built for MENA

Emily Rodriguez. Sarah Johnson. Sarah Johnson again. The homepage promises "Built in Cairo. Designed for the entire Arab world" and "mentors who understand your market" — then the mentor list contains nobody from the region. Whether these are seed rows or real, the effect is the same: your core differentiator is contradicted on the page where it matters most. Fix: if it is placeholder data, replace it today with real MENA mentors, even five. If it is real, recruit regionally before promoting this feature.

M-04 P0

No timezone on any session

"10:00 AM" across a region spanning UTC+1 (Morocco) to UTC+4 (UAE). Fix: render in the founder's timezone and name it explicitly — "1:30 PM your time (GMT+3)". Missed calls from timezone ambiguity are the standard failure mode of every mentoring product.

M-05 P1

"pending" and "upcoming" are never explained

Two states, both green, both with a tick icon, no legend, no action attached. Does pending need something from the founder? Fix: distinct colours and shapes per state, plus an action when one is required — "Waiting for Sarah to confirm" with a nudge button.

M-06 P1

The cards are inert

No join link, no add-to-calendar, no reschedule, no cancel, no session length, no language, no mentor photo, and nothing appears clickable. Fix: each card carries the four things a booking must have — when (with timezone), how long, in what language, and one button to join or manage.

M-07 P2

No preparation, no follow-up

Fix: auto-send the mentor a one-page brief built from the founder's mission progress 24h before, and show the founder three suggested questions. Afterwards, capture three action items and push them into the current mission. This is the cheapest possible upgrade to perceived mentor quality.

SCREEN 05

Journey history

Verdict · The best-executed screen of the five

Correct empty-state anatomy: icon, plain heading, one line explaining what will appear here, one clear action. This is the standard the other four should be held to.

J-01 P1

A manual Refresh button admits the data is stale

If the user has to refresh, the app is telling them it does not know when its own data changed. Fix: refetch on focus and after any submission, and remove the button. If it must stay, it belongs as a small icon, not a bordered control at the same weight as the page title.

J-02 P1

Three names for overlapping ideas

"Dashboard", "My Startup Journey", "Journey history" — plus "Loadout" and "AI Tools" nearby. A founder cannot predict which one holds what. Fix: Dashboard = today's next action. Journey = the 15 missions. History = past submissions, and it should live as a tab inside Journey rather than as its own destination.

J-03 P1

Two words for one object in two consecutive lines

The subtitle says "Every recap you submitted"; the empty state says "No submissions yet". Fix: pick one word and use it in the nav label, the page title, the empty state, and the button. Vocabulary drift is how a product starts feeling improvised.

J-04 P2

The empty state could sell the feature

Fix: show a greyed-out sample recap card beneath the message so the founder can see what they will get — the answers, the AI score, the evidence. Empty states that demonstrate value outperform empty states that merely explain absence.

Across all five screens

The chrome is eating the product

X-01 P0

Roughly 180px of fixed chrome before any content, on every page

An 88px navigation bar plus a 90px profile-completion banner, present on all five screens including the marketing homepage. On a laptop that is a quarter of the viewport; on a phone it would be close to half. Fix: the banner must be dismissible for the session and must never appear on the public landing page.

X-02 P0

Nine top-level navigation items, one of them "Sign out"

Dashboard · My Startup Journey · Resources · Loadout · AI Tools · More ▾ · language · notifications · name · Sign out. Sign out is the action a user wants roughly once a month, sitting permanently at the same weight as the core product, one target away from their own name. Fix: five items maximum, and move Sign out into the avatar menu. On mobile this becomes a bottom tab bar of five.

X-03 P1

"Loadout" does not communicate

It is gaming vocabulary — the kit you equip before a match. As a Gen Z-native metaphor it is a defensible choice, but nobody can guess what it contains, and it sits next to plain labels like Resources and AI Tools, so it reads as an inconsistency rather than a wink. Fix: either rename it to what it holds, or keep the name and add a one-line description on hover and in the mobile menu.

X-04 P1

The completion banner nags instead of solving

Country, Industry, Startup stage — three dropdowns, about thirty seconds of work, presented as a persistent progress bar with a checklist, a snooze and a dismiss. Fix: put the three fields inline in the banner. Three taps and it is gone forever. You are currently spending permanent screen space to defer a half-minute task.

X-05 P1

One green doing eleven jobs

Primary buttons, active nav, links, stat figures, tag chips, status badges, icons, progress fill and section eyebrows are all the same green. When everything is emphasised, nothing leads — which is why the red Delete button wins every screen it appears on. Fix: reserve full-strength green for the single primary action per screen. Everything else drops to a tint, an outline, or plain text.

X-06 P1

Pages end in a cliff of empty space

Mentoring, Community and the Collaboration Hub all stop abruptly with half a screen or more of nothing. Fix: every page ends with a next step — on Mentoring, suggested mentors; on Community, prompts to post; on the Hub, projects seeking your skills. Never let a page terminate in void.

X-07 P1

The floating chat bubble overlaps content

On Community it sits on top of the Trending Tags card. Fix: give it a safe-area offset and let it shrink or hide on scroll. On mobile it must not collide with a bottom tab bar.

X-08 P1

Two language switchers, and no visible Arabic UI

"ENGLISH" in the nav, "English" in the footer, different casing. Meanwhile the Community post carries a per-post translate toggle — so content translation exists while interface translation is unproven. Fix: one switcher, and confirm that choosing Arabic actually flips the entire interface to RTL. Given the audience, this is the highest-value thing on this list after the bugs.

X-09 P2

"More ▾" is styled as permanently active

It carries a filled pill on every screen, including ones whose page lives under it — so the navigation never indicates which page you are actually on. Fix: active state belongs on the current destination, not on the container.

X-10 P2

What works — keep it

The typography is consistent and well-set across every screen, the green identity is coherent and un-templated, empty states exist (Journey history is genuinely good), and the per-post translate feature is a real competitive edge. The craft is present; it is priority and information architecture that need the work.

R02 · Navigation & chrome diet
The logged-in shell has roughly 180px of fixed chrome before any content — an 88px nav plus a 90px profile banner — on every page. Fix it.

NAVIGATION
- Reduce to 5 top-level items: Dashboard, Journey, Community, Mentors, Resources. Everything else goes into the avatar menu.

PROFILE BANNER

R03 · Fix Arabic post rendering
Arabic posts in the Community feed render inside a left-to-right container. The Arabic body text is right-aligned but the card around it is not — the category chip, author row, timestamp, hashtags, and view/like/comment icons all stay LTR. The hashtag chip renders the "#" on the wrong side with a stranded full stop.

FIX
- Detect the dominant script of each post at render and set dir="rtl" or dir="ltr" on the ENTIRE post card, not just the text node.
- All internal spacing must use logical CSS properties (margin-inline-start, padding-inline-end, text-align: start) so the card mirrors correctly as a unit.
- Hashtags in Arabic posts render with "#" leading in reading order — the RTL container handles this once direction is set on the right element.
- Wrap mixed-direction fragments (an English product name inside an Arabic sentence) in  so they don't scramble the surrounding line.
- Arabic body text needs its own type treatment: roughly 1.1x the font size and 1.2x the line-height of the Latin equivalent, using an Arabic-native family (IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Noto Sans Arabic, or Cairo).
- Keep the existing "Translate to English" toggle — it's excellent — and add the reverse for English posts when the viewer's locale is Arabic.

Test with the existing post "الأغذيه الصحيه من الأرض للفضاء" and with a post mixing Arabic and English.

R08 · Colour hierarchy pass
One green is doing eleven jobs across StartPad — primary buttons, active nav, links, stat figures, tag chips, status badges, icons, progress fills and section eyebrows are all the same saturated green. When everything is emphasised nothing leads, which is why a red Delete button currently wins every screen it's on.

ESTABLISH A HIERARCHY
- Full-strength green: exactly ONE primary action per screen. Nothing else.
- Secondary actions: outlined, green text on transparent.
- Tertiary actions: plain text, no container.
- Tag chips, eyebrows and metadata: neutral grey, not green.
- Stat figures: ink colour, not green. Let the number carry the weight through size and a monospace tabular face.
- Semantic colours (success / warning / danger) are a separate set from the brand green and are used ONLY for state, never for emphasis.

DESTRUCTIVE ACTIONS
Never a filled primary button. Text or outline in danger colour, in an overflow menu, with a confirm dialog naming the consequence.

Audit every screen after the change: there should be exactly one obvious "what do I do here" target per page. Also verify 4.5:1 contrast on white text over the green — check the actual value, it's a common failure at this saturation.
R09 · No page ends in emptiness
Mentoring, Community and the Collaboration Hub all end abruptly with half a screen or more of blank space. Every page must end with a next step.

- Mentoring: after the session list, show "Mentors available this week" matched to the founder's stage and country.
- Community: after the feed, show mission-linked prompts to post, and founders on the same mission to follow.
- Collaboration Hub: after the project grid, show "Projects looking for your skills" based on the founder's profile.
- Journey history (empty): beneath the existing empty state, show a greyed-out sample recap card so the founder can see what they'll get — the answers, the AI score, the evidence. Demonstrating the value beats explaining the absence.

Also fix the floating chat bubble: it currently overlaps the Trending Tags card on Community. Give it a safe-area offset, shrink or hide it on scroll, and ensure it never collides with a mobile bottom tab bar.
R10 · Information architecture cleanup
StartPad currently has overlapping destinations that founders can't distinguish. Consolidate.

CURRENT OVERLAPS
- "Dashboard" vs "My Startup Journey" vs "Journey history" — three names, unclear boundaries.
- "My Sessions" in the Collaboration Hub vs sessions on the Mentoring page — two homes for scheduled things.
- "Team Chat" in the Collaboration Hub vs Community — two homes for conversation.

TARGET STRUCTURE
- Dashboard = what to do right now. One next-action card, then progress.
- Journey = the 15 missions, with "History" as a TAB inside it, not a separate destination.
- Mentors = find, book and manage every mentor session. The only home for scheduled 1:1s.
- Community = all public conversation. Project team chat stays inside its project.
- Projects = the Collaboration Hub, with Team / Files / Chat as tabs inside each project.

VOCABULARY
Pick one word per concept and use it in the nav label, page title, empty state and buttons. Currently "recap" and "submissions" describe the same object in two consecutive lines on Journey history. Build a short glossary and enforce it across all copy.
R11 · Verify Arabic UI end to end
StartPad has a language switcher labelled ENGLISH, and per-post content translation in Community — but I need the full interface verified in Arabic.

Switch the app to Arabic and check every screen, then fix what breaks:
- Does the entire interface translate, or only some strings? List anything still in English.
- Does dir="rtl" apply to the whole document — nav, banner, cards, forms, dropdowns, modals, toasts?
- Are directional icons mirrored (arrows, chevrons, progress fills, the mission path)? Logos, clocks and phone numbers must NOT mirror.
- Is an Arabic-native font loaded (IBM Plex Sans Arabic / Noto Sans Arabic / Cairo) at its own scale — ~1.1x size and ~1.2x line-height versus Latin? Never rely on a Latin font's Arabic fallback.
- Are dates, numbers and currency formatted per locale via Intl?
- Do mixed-direction strings (an English startup name in an Arabic sentence) render correctly with ?
- Is the locale in the URL (/ar/*), persisted per user, with hreflang tags?
- Consolidate the two language switchers (nav "ENGLISH", footer "English") into one.

Report every screen that breaks. Given this audience, half-working RTL is worse than English-only.
R12 · Mobile audit
Every StartPad screen I've reviewed is desktop, but the audience is overwhelmingly on phones. Audit and fix the full app at 390px width.

- The logged-in shell currently has ~180px of fixed chrome (88px nav + 90px profile banner). On a phone that's close to half the viewport. Collapse the nav to a bottom tab bar with 5 items and make the banner a single slim dismissible line.
- Convert every centred modal to a bottom sheet, draggable to dismiss.
- The Collaboration Hub's 5-button grid and the Community 4-card stat row must reflow, not shrink.
- The Mentoring session cards must stack with time, timezone and the primary action all visible without horizontal scroll.
- Every tap target 44x44px minimum, with spacing between adjacent targets. The Delete button in particular must not sit adjacent to a common action.
- The floating chat bubble must not overlap the bottom tab bar or any primary action.
- No horizontal page scroll anywhere.
- Test the landing page: it's currently very long on desktop and will be enormous on mobile. Compress it and add a sticky bottom CTA.

Report each screen with a before/after and list what you changed.
